Method, probability rules & contrarian rules

Where the numbers come from

  • HotSpawn is used for fixture discovery and pick'em points only; its crowd % is shown as ownership and is never a win probability.
  • Win probabilities come only from external two-sided betting lines already captured on the repo's market boards (Melbet, Kalshi, Thunderpick via bo3.gg), cross-checked against a second published line where one exists.
  • Where no two-sided line was retrieved, the probability is left blank (never 50/50, never invented) and the row is published in the Unresolved picks & limitations section with the source attempts.
  • Editorial predictions (e.g. the official HotSpawn Day 3 preview) are labelled editorial and are never converted into a probability.

Resolution ladder for a match with no featured market pick

  1. HLTV first — event/match/analytics pages for fixtures, results, rankings and form.
  2. Then esportsbooks and exchanges — the repo market boards' captured two-sided lines (Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Kalshi, Thunderpick via bo3.gg); a second published line is used as a cross-check, and suspected placeholder quotes (e.g. uniform 1.80/1.80) are rejected.
  3. Then an originated estimate — only from uniquely identified official Valve VRS ratings, clearly labelled as our model, never presented as a bookmaker price.
  4. Otherwise blank — published on the Unresolved page with the attempts and the limitation.

Contrarian-but-not-stupid rules (leaderboard picks)

  1. Start from the external market probability, never from the result you want. The safe pick is always the higher verified probability.
  2. A leaderboard (contrarian) pick must clear three gates: (1) verified win probability at least 25% — a live underdog, not a lottery ticket; (2) low HotSpawn ownership, so a correct call gains on the field (leverage = win % ÷ ownership %); (3) a concrete, dated reason the underdog's chance is real (same-event head-to-head, current-event form, a verified roster/rest edge) — not just 'favourites sometimes lose'.
  3. Recent roster changes cut both ways: a new IGL/coach/language (Aurora, July 2026) is a reason to check integration evidence, not an automatic fade — especially 100 days and a top-3 EPL finish later. Count a roster or rest factor only when it is dated, sourced, and not already reflected in the market line.
  4. Cap the number of contrarian swings per day and never spend one where there is no verified probability or no ownership data (BetBoom vs Legacy fails both gates today).
  5. Grade picks after the event with the pre-lock probability logged (calibration over many matches), not with post-match stories. Day 1's 9z win proves the crowd can over-own a favourite; it does not prove any single Day 3 underdog will win.
