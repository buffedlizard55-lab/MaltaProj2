Fallback policy

  1. Preserve featured picks backed by eligible, correctly matched, two-sided external lines.
  2. For uncovered fixtures, inspect HLTV for roster and result context, then bookmakers/exchanges for exact-event prices.
  3. Originate a model only with confirmed same-team inputs and disclose its calibration, date and assumptions. A rating is not a quoted probability.
  4. If identity, timestamp, market scope or input evidence cannot be confirmed, keep the probability blank and publish the limitation. Do not borrow another roster's rating to satisfy coverage.

The existing collectors and historical alternate-board data have not been rewritten by this audit. Their provisional values remain accessible for review. The discrepancies above are explicitly unresolved rather than certified.
