Method, formulas and every source read

What this audit is

An independent, line-by-line re-verification of the published HotSpawn CS2 pick boards — the every-match strategy board, its day-1 review and backtest, and the two contested team identities — performed by session 6c5bb3d6 against official sources only. It does not replace any board; it re-derives their numbers from raw evidence and publishes what agrees, what diverges and what could not be confirmed.

Formulas

  • Proportional no-vig. From a two-sided decimal line, p_A = (1/d_A) / (1/d_A + 1/d_B). Every retained raw quote in this repository was re-derived with this formula and compared with the published market probability (tolerance ±6 points at the row mean, and the row must fall inside the quote range).
  • Leaderboard differential. edge_X = points × (p_X − c_X) where c_X is the HotSpawn crowd ownership of side X. Derivation: your point margin against the field is points × [(1−c)·1{X wins} − c·1{X loses}], whose expectation is points × (p − c). The differential-optimal pick is argmax of that edge.
  • Official-standings model (reference only). p = 1 / (1 + 10^(−(R_A − R_B)/400)) on Valve's published regional-standings points. The 400-point scale is a stated modelling assumption, not a Valve-published quantity; it is used to expose divergence from the market, never to overwrite a market price.

Source policy

  • HotSpawn (play.hotspawn.com/lobby) is fixture discovery and settlement only. Its crowd pick percentage is published as ownership for the differential arithmetic and is never an input to a win probability — check C6 proves the published probabilities are identical to the upstream market/model values.
  • Prices come from licensed bookmakers and exchanges: Pinnacle (public guest odds API), Bovada (public coupon JSON), the Melbet/1xBet-platform LineFeed, Kalshi, and the Thunderpick coefficients bo3.gg publishes per fixture.
  • Ratings come from Valve's own publication, ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings (snapshot 2026_10_05, fetched in this run over the GitHub contents API: HTTP 200, 349 teams).
  • Results, rosters and context come from HLTV match/news/event/team pages, Liquipedia, and named esports press, each recorded with its URL and UTC read time in data/6c5bb3d6/research.json.
  • Rejected sources are recorded with the reason — egamersworld's uniform 1.80/1.80 placeholder prices and HLTV's fan vote are never used as probabilities.
