Method & provenance

This section audits, rather than replaces, the f7279a54 strict board. It publishes an external-market estimate only after independently recomputing it from the preserved raw snapshot.

1. Discover fixtures

Use page-listed open HotSpawn CS2 fixtures from the board. HotSpawn links are retained per row. No crowd ownership or HotSpawn pick percentage is read, stored, or used.

2. Re-derive odds

Raw two-way bookmaker prices are converted to decimal odds when needed. Each implied probability is divided by the two implied probabilities' sum, removing the quoted overround.

3. Match conservatively

Both teams and a verified start must match. Normalization is limited to case, diacritics, punctuation, and generic organization suffixes. No fuzzy or substring matching is allowed.

4. Exact reorder exception

When only full team-token order differs (for example, “Apogee Betclic” / “Betclic Apogee”), both teams' complete token multisets, opponent, and source start must match. Such rows are visibly flagged.

5. Consensus and pick

Source no-vig probabilities are weighted Pinnacle 2, Bovada 1, Melbet 1. The displayed pick is the higher resulting probability; it is a deterministic label, not advice.

6. Stop on uncertainty

In-play, approximate-time, stale, ambiguous, alias-only, or unverified data cannot create a probability. The row is blank and linked into the unresolved review.

Fallback ladder & limitations

  1. Try HLTV as a match/research check. For this input it was blocked; it is recorded below, not treated as a source.
  2. Use externally captured sportsbook/exchange odds only when exact join rules pass.
  3. Do not originate a VRS/model-only line here: the retained VRS snapshot is flagged stale or unverified for every current fallback context. Blank is safer and required by the audit policy.
