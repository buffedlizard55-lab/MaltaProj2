Probability calculation: FURIA vs Aurora

The decimal line is converted to implied probability, then normalized to remove the two-way overround. No additional roster, recency, fatigue or crowd adjustment is applied.

implied = 1 / decimal odds
overround = (1 / odds A) + (1 / odds B) − 1
no-vig P(A) = (1 / odds A) / ((1 / odds A) + (1 / odds B))

Decision rule and tie handling

  • When two verified source readings both normalize to 50/50, publish the supported probabilities but leave the directional team pick blank. A forced side is arbitrary; missing ownership makes contrarian expected points uncomputable.
  • When a team or opponent is a tournament-page placeholder, do not emit a probability or pick until official pairing and relevant price are confirmed.
  • Roster announcements can be verified without proving a causal win-probability effect. No numerical adjustment is made for Aurora’s July additions.
  • Actual rest hours were not verified; match count is not converted into fatigue probability.
  • For other CS2 fixtures, use the repository’s all-match board and its dedicated unresolved page; this TWC preview does not overwrite their method or counts.
