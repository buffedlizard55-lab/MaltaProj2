Line-by-line checks

  1. Load every open fixture from the canonical board and parse every rendered row in picks-every-match/picks.html.
  2. Join by fixture ID. Compare basis, both displayed percentages, rendered pick, deadline, and source-link presence.
  3. For priced rows, check finite probabilities sum to one within 0.02 percentage points and the pick matches the maximum side. Market rows are compared to retained market probabilities; model rows to retained model probabilities.
  4. For unresolved rows, require no numeric probability and no rendered pick. The absence is reported as unresolved; it is not turned into 50/50.
