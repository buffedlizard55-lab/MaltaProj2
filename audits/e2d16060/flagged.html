Unresolved fixture rule

An unresolved row is not a failure. If HLTV, a book, an exchange, or Valve VRS cannot provide defensible identity, kickoff, and price evidence, the canonical row remains blank and links to its evidence flag. This is the only honest way to meet the no-hallucination constraint.

Audit evidence is dated and offline-replayed. A source URL is a manual-review link, not a claim that this run fetched it. HotSpawn supplies fixture discovery only; its crowd ownership never enters the win-probability arithmetic.
