1. Why Strict Primary-Bookmaker Boards Leave 12 Open Matches Unresolved

  1. Lead-Time Market Delay on Tier-C Regional Qualifiers (>24–30h Before Kickoff): Primary sharp books (Pinnacle, Bovada) post Tier-C BO1 group-stage lines (Elisa BreakOut Series #2 Group B on Oct 16) and online closed qualifiers (CCT Europe Series #12 Closed Qualifier on Oct 16) 6–18 hours before match start. Secondary esportsfeeds (Melbet, Thunderpick via bo3.gg) and Kalshi open some Group B markets earlier, while 3 matches (HAVU vs RoundsGG, Phantom vs RoundsGG, mellren vs Privateer) have no bookmaker market open yet and require originated CS2 lines.
  2. Team Rebrands & Abbreviation Aliases (Solved 5-for-5 via Today's HLTV Match Pages): HotSpawn lists full or sponsor names (WROTBERRY, RoundsGG) while HLTV, Valve VRS, Pinnacle, and Thunderpick list WBT and ROUNDS. Prior strict sessions left WROTBERRY and RoundsGG blank because Kalshi labeled an earlier slot WBT Academy and HLTV's ROUNDS team page only listed 4 players yesterday. Today's official HLTV match pages (2399420 and 2399425) publish all 5 starters for both teams, matching Valve's official 2026-10-05 VRS standings (#60 WBT and #310 ROUNDS) 5-for-5.
  3. Active Roster Substitutions & BO1 Variance: Even when resolved via fallback esportsbooks or Valve VRS, Tier-C BO1 LAN matches carry higher variance than Tier-S BO3s, and HLTV discloses active stand-ins (qx for Fessor on EAC; cej0t for Kunai on Phantom) that post-date the 2026-10-05 Valve VRS snapshot.
