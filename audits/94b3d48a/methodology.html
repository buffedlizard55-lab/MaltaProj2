Methodology, Source Hierarchy & 4-Hour Automated Refresh

How we generate source-verified picks and win probabilities for every CS2 match on HotSpawn without ever using HotSpawn's crowd pick percentage or fabricating unverified quotes.

1. Strict 3-Tier Source Hierarchy (Fallbacks Used ONLY When Primary Route Cannot Resolve)

Tier When Invoked Sources & Mathematical Formula Official Verification Anchor
Tier 1 · Featured Primary Bookmakers
(8 open matches)		 Always tried first. Used whenever Pinnacle or Bovada lists a prematch series/match-winner line with exact team & UTC kickoff match. Proportional no-vig normalization: q_i = 1 / odds_i, p_i = q_i / (q_a + q_b). Multi-book consensus uses the arithmetic mean of per-book no-vig probabilities. Fallback sources are never invoked on Tier 1 matches.

Tier 2 · Fallback Esportsbooks & Exchanges + HLTV
(5 open matches)		 Invoked only when a fixture is not resolved by Tier 1 strict primary-bookmaker matching. Step 2a verifies team identity and 5/5 starting lineups on official HLTV match pages (resolving WROTBERRY = WBT #60 VRS and RoundsGG = ROUNDS #310 VRS). Step 2b computes the no-vig probability from alias-verified Pinnacle lines, Melbet LineFeed, Thunderpick (bo3.gg), and Kalshi CFTC exchange quotes.
HLTV Analytics bo3.gg / Thunderpick Kalshi CS2
Tier 3 · Originated CS2 Lines (Valve VRS + HLTV + Elo)
(4 open matches)		 Invoked only when neither primary bookmakers nor fallback esportsbooks/exchanges have opened a prematch market yet. Map win probability from official Valve VRS points: p_map = 1 / (1 + 10^(-(Pts_A - Pts_B) / 600)). For BO3 series: p_bo3 = p_map^2 * (3 - 2 * p_map). Fair decimal line: d_i = 1 / p_i. Cross-checked against HLTV world rank, recent form, and 809-match historical Elo.
