Our two-track framework separates Safe (Maximum Expected Points) picks from Contrarian Leaderboard Swing picks:

  • Gate 1 (Market Floor ≥ 35.0%): Never pick a hopeless longshot. The underdog must hold at least a 35.0% no-vig win probability across ≥2 primary bookmakers.
