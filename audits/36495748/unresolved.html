Standing limitations

L1 — Tier-C events are priced late or never. Low-tier CS2 (CIS LAN, CCT Challengers, regional cups) is routinely absent from Polymarket and from the books the collectors read (Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Thunderpick-via-bo3, Kalshi). Today that applied to 1082762 (ex-CYBERSHOKE–BlinkDagger: no price at any of them; bo3.gg does not even list the CIS LAN Season 9 fixture) and 1082837 (Zetta–LOS NINOS: listed on bo3.gg with bet_updates null, i.e. Thunderpick publishes no coefficient). The desk falls back to official ratings for these, and says so on the row.

L2 — The official rating file is monthly. Valve's newest global standings file is 2026_10_05 — 16 days old today and still the latest published (repo HEAD eec4853, verified 2026-10-21). Model rows inherit that staleness: roster moves after 5 October are not in the numbers. HLTV's own VRS display (e.g. The Huns #125 on 2026-10-21 vs its #122 row in the Oct 5 file) shows the small drift this creates.

L3 — Valve lists multiple rows under one team name. The official file keeps historical cores: e.g. two 'The Huns' rows (#122/#213), two 'Banda Chuya' rows (#165/#373), two 'CYBERSHOKE' rows (#61/#171). Joining by name alone is unsafe — an upstream board priced two of today's fixtures off dead rosters (see Flagged F1/F2). This desk joins a duplicate name only with current-lineup evidence (≥3 of 5 players), otherwise the model rung refuses.

L4 — Renames and 'ex-' teams. 'Zetta' is now Alianza Lima Esports (identical five); 'ex-CYBERSHOKE' is a post-org core. Names on HotSpawn, books and rating files drift apart, so identity is proven by roster overlap, not by string similarity. Similar-looking but different teams (e.g. CYBERSHOKE Prospects) are never used as proxies.

L5 — Thin or single-source prices. Some fixtures are priced by exactly one thin market (today: Kalshi's $1,440-volume book on UPGRADE–Team flacons). These are used, clearly badged, and should be trusted less than a multi-book consensus; exchange coin flips are broken by the official rating and say so.

L6 — HLTV access is best-effort. HLTV is the project's main identity/form source, but it sometimes returns 403 to automated collectors ('hltv_probe' fails in upstream runs). Automated refreshes only prove machine-checkable rungs; when a fixture needs HLTV lineup reasoning it lands in Unresolved until the identity work is done and recorded.

L7 — The HotSpawn lobby itself can be stale or flicker. Fixture 1080200 (Wildcard–DENDELE) appears in one upstream capture and not another, as already observed on 2026-10-16. The desk shows presence per capture and flags single-capture rows instead of silently trusting either.

L8 — Model numbers are estimates, not prices. VRS-model and directional rows come from a curve fitted to market lines (upstream fit: 60 pairs, MAE 7.7pp). Expect mistakes of that order; a 76.6% model row is not comparable in quality to a 76.6% three-book market row, which is exactly why every row carries its basis badge.
