The ladder

RungBasis badgeRule
1🎯 MarketRe-derive a weighted no-vig consensus from the raw two-way prices stored on the strict market board (Pinnacle weight 2, others 1 — the upstream-published weights). Every quote is re-oriented from the names printed by its own source; a quote that doesn't match the fixture pair, starts >1.5h off the HotSpawn start, or doesn't reproduce its stored probability is excluded. The recomputed consensus must agree with the board's stored consensus (≤0.6pp), and the desk publishes its own recomputed number.
2🔁 ExchangeKalshi event whose start is proven (event-page read: the EDT time converts exactly to the HotSpawn UTC start; or runner probe with ticker+name match). Probability = no-vig of the two Yes mid prices. A result within 1pp of 50% is a coin flip: probabilities stand as read, the pick is taken from the higher official Valve rating and labelled as a tiebreak.
3🏅 VRS modelBoth teams resolve to exactly one row of Valve's official global standings. Duplicate names require current-roster evidence (≥3 of 5 players shared with the row, from HLTV/bo3 lineups) — otherwise the rung refuses. Probability from the formula below.
4🧭 DirectionalExactly one team is officially rated and the other has no row at all in the official file (searched exact + token, variants recorded). The rated team is priced against the table's last row (#380, 381 pts). Clearly badged: directional, not a market price.
5⚠️ UnresolvedBlank probabilities, rung-by-rung ledger on Unresolved & limitations. Never filled by guesswork, name similarity, crowd data or another roster used as a proxy.

Formulas

  • No-vig (proportional): for decimals d1, d2: qi=1/di, pi=qi/(q1+q2). Books totalling outside [0.95, 1.30] are rejected as bad data.
  • Official-rating model: p(a) = 1/(1+exp(−k·C·(ptsa−ptsb))) with C = g·ln(10)/400 = 0.005600 from Valve's own model code (counter-strike_regional_standings, glicko.js: 400 pts ≙ 90%, RD 75). Current k = 0.430779 — fitted upstream calibration in data/f7279a54/board.json (least-squares fit of logit(market) = k·C·Δ; calibration block: {"n_pairs": 60, "k": 0.43077894236569164, "fitted": true, "k_raw": 0.43077894236569164, "mae_prob": 0.07702503405478646, "mae_prob_valve": 0.12247910190606133, "min_pairs": 6, "bounds": [0.25, 1.5], "pairs_used": 60}).

Verification gates (build aborts on failure)

  • Every market quote recomputes from its decimals and orients by its own printed names; no price link may point at HotSpawn; consensus must match the upstream board's within 0.6pp.
  • Every model/directional row recomputes from the official points for the cited standings rows (checked against the pinned file, sha256 df96e7fc3cd91a26…).
  • Probabilities sum to 1; the pick is always the higher-probability team unless a documented coin-flip tiebreak says otherwise; unresolved rows carry no pick.
  • HotSpawn crowd/ownership, HLTV's community vote and bo3.gg's analytics percentage are structurally absent from every formula.

Freshness automation

Upstream collectors refresh the fixture/quote boards as often as ~27 minutes; this desk re-derives and republishes about every 4 hours (.github/workflows/36495748-refresh.yml): it re-probes Kalshi and bo3.gg for fixtures still lacking prices, checks Valve's repo for a newer standings file, re-runs the ladder and all verification gates, and only then commits. Deep identity reasoning (HLTV lineups) is recorded manually in data/36495748/overrides.json so automation can never silently invent it. Every page header prints the input capture times — if upstream stalls, the desk stops claiming freshness instead of recycling old numbers.
