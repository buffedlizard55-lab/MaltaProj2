Method & calibration

The seven published rules, the slope fit, and the complete VRS join ledger. Everything here is recomputed from retained evidence; nothing is inherited from a prior board.

The seven published rules

  1. Blank policy. a probability is published only when derivable from a retained source; otherwise the cell stays blank and the fixture is ledgered as unresolved
  2. Market consensus. median of per-source no-vig probabilities
  3. Valve VRS model. Valve Glicko expected-score formula, RD=75.0 (Valve's fit.js default), slope k fitted against joined market lines
  4. Elo (last resort). plain Elo over retained bo3.gg history, k=20.0, 8 chronological passes, VRS teams anchored at their Valve points
  5. Published line. 0.85 market / 0.15 model when both exist — market median when at least one usable price exists, else the model, else BLANK.
  6. Crowd policy. HotSpawn pick'em crowd share is context only and is never a probability input
  7. Seed policy. untrained seed prices (exact 1.80/1.80 placeholders with zero votes, $0-volume markets, wide bid/ask seeds, overround > 0.30) are rejected, not averaged

Calibration — our own fit

Valve's README states the published curve is too steep at both ends, so the slope is learned from observed bookmaker lines instead of assuming k=1. Fit: least squares through the origin on ln(market/(1-market)) = k · C · (pointsa − pointsb), C = g·ln10/400 with g = 1/sqrt(1 + 3Q²RD²/π²), RD = 75 (Valve's fit.js default).

k = 0.4970 (raw 0.4970, unclamped, inside band, band [0.1, 1.5], minimum 6 pairs), n = 19 pairs, MAE(prob) = 0.0592. fe803b80 published k=0.275, MAE 0.1002 on 19 pairs. Our slope is steeper (0.497 vs 0.275) because the live Oct-16 ladder compressed several mid-tier deltas (TWC point swings and 128-day decay) while the markets held — and our MAE is lower (0.059 vs 0.100), i.e. the fit is better, not just different.
