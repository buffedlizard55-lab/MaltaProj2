What it teaches about climbing the leaderboard

  • The crowd routinely puts 88–96% on favourites the market prices at 70–75% (Aurora 96% crowd vs ~70% market; Fnatic 92% vs ~53%).
  • Those are the cheapest places to separate: an underdog the market rates as live, that almost nobody owns.
  • But not every underdog: the rules skip dogs below 25% and dogs the crowd already likes, and cap big swings at 3 a day.
