Research order used for these matches

  1. HLTV (team, match and analytics pages) for identity, rosters and ranking — HLTV's "Best odds" fields were empty for these low-tier matches when read, and HLTV blocks automated runners (403), so HLTV is identity/context only.
  2. Esportsbooks and betting lines: Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet/1xBet LineFeed, Thunderpick coefficients via bo3.gg, Kalshi exchange — collected automatically by the repository's collectors.
  3. Originated line: official Valve VRS points and bo3.gg match-history Elo, only where no price exists.
