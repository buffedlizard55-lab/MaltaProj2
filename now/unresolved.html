Limitations, stated plainly

  1. Coverage of the low tiers. Only about a third of this week's CS2 lobby is priced by a book we can read. bo3.gg lists a winner market for 12 matches in its whole upcoming set; the VOID Autumn Cup, NODWIN Clutch Series and the Brazilian tier-C cards render 'All odds are on' with no 1/2 pair at all. Absence of a pair is treated as 'unpriced', never as a fetch failure.
  2. HLTV is not a price source here. HLTV's betting pages for these fixtures publish prose ('Falcons is the bookmaker favorite with the best odds') and no numeric moneyline. Used as a direction check only.
  3. Ratings lag by 13 days. The newest file in Valve's official repo is 2026_10_05 and the snapshot cadence is monthly, so no TWC result is in it. HLTV's event page even seeds the event off the Aug 3 VRS. The model component is capped at 15% weight for that reason and is the whole input for rows flagged 'rating model only'.
  4. Duplicate names in the official standings. Valve keeps a row per lineup, so 'chinggis_warriors' and 'betclic' each appear twice with very different points. Two joins were wrong on the shared board and are corrected here; a fix request is logged on flagged for review.
  5. Crowd data is too thin to be useful on the big cards. The two TWC day-5 cards carried up to 22 picks in the API at the read below, against a 20-pick floor. The floor is now met, so the leverage figures on the day-5 page are live.
  6. Small samples elsewhere. Where the crowd sample does clear 20 picks it is on tier-B and tier-C cards with no cards at all picks. That is enough to describe ownership, not enough to estimate how the rest of the pool will pick, so leverage figures are shown as points relative to the field, not as a chance of moving up a leaderboard.
  7. Nothing is modelled for a specific map. The board is match-winner only. HotSpawn's own card text on some fixtures says the pick is for the series winner; no map veto, handicap or alternate market is priced here beyond the alt lines quoted as context on the day-5 page.
