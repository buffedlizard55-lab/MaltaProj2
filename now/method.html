Method, rules and read ledger

Rules this page is built under

  • Market first, model second. A two-way decimal pair is devigged proportionally: p(A) = (1/odds A) / (1/odds A + 1/odds B). A book whose pair sums outside 0.95-1.30 implied is discarded. Books are weighted Pinnacle 2.0, Bovada 1.5, Thunderpick 1.5, Kalshi 1.0, Melbet 1.0.
  • Blend. line = sigmoid(w x logit(market) + (1-w) x logit(model)), w = 0.85 with two or more sources and 0.75 with one. With no rating pair for one side the line is the market alone; with no price it is the model alone and gets flagged.
  • Rating model. p(A) = sigmoid(k x C x (ptsA - ptsB)) using Valve's own arithmetic (400 rank points = 90%, RD 75 as in model/fit.js), with the fitted slope k = 0.4046 from 15 priced pairs on this board. Labels: Strong at 70%+, Solid 60%+, Lean 53%+, Toss-up below.
  • Contrarian floor. Never recommend switching to a side the priced line puts under 35%, whatever the crowd is doing. Leverage figures are only printed when the crowd sample has at least 20 picks and our line differs from the crowd by at least 10pp, and are reported as points relative to the field: points x (our probability - crowd share of that side).
  • HotSpawn is a fixture and ownership source only. Its percentages are never an input to a line, and a card whose only evidence is crowd behaviour gets no pick from this desk.
  • A model line above 85% gets demoted to a direction. Where no book prices a card and the rating or Elo model is very confident, the number is printed with a flag: those models are measurably worse at the extremes than at the middle, so 95.1% on a tier-C card means "play it", not "95% of the time".
  • Blank beats wrong. If no verified source supports a number, the cell is blank, the reason is published, and the sources that were tried are named.
  • Read times are part of the data. Every price carries the minute it was read; nothing on this page is older than the board read in the header.

Refresh

GitHub Actions runs the collector and this builder every four hours (workflow now-b3871d16.yml), and the staging step re-renders before every Pages publish, so the HTML on this site can never be older than the board it is derived from. The collector only ever adds prices it can prove; a failed fetch leaves the previous file in place and is reported in the header as "collector: failed".

Track record - and why this desk does not quote one

The shared board reports 6908 settled picks, 5257 won, Brier 0.1922, and per-basis accuracies of market 90.6% / market+model 75.7% / model 69.2%. Those per-basis figures are not usable: the history holds one row per refresh for each fixture (4,870 rows for 107 fixtures) and every "won" row has pick == winner by construction, so a 99% model-only hit rate is a bookkeeping artefact, not a result. See IR-1. The only calibration this page relies on is the fitted VRS slope, with a mean absolute error of 10.8pp against no-vig book lines over 15 pairs - i.e. our rating-only rows should be read as plus or minus about ten points.
