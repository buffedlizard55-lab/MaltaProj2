What the first four days mean for day 5

  1. Favorites are not safe at this LAN. Aurora were the only favorite to drop a match on day 1 and Falcons - the best-ranked team in the event - went out 1-2 to BETBOOM in the semi. Two of the eight series above were won by the side the market liked less. That argues for flat, unforced picks on the favorites in the low-tier cards, not for chasing dogs.
  2. Aurora answered the upset immediately. 2-0 over NiP, then 2-0 over 9z in the decider with kyxsan at 1.45, then a 0-2 loss to FURIA in the semi. The rebuild is not a live edge; it is a settled body of work - five events and a first final together by 19 September.
  3. BETBOOM is playing the best hockey-stick of the event. 0-2 to FURIA on day 1, then three straight series wins including the semifinal, 11 maps played to FURIA's 7. The rest cost is the one thing a price may not have fully taken on board, and it points at FURIA, not against it.
  4. The map veto is not knowable in advance here. HLTV's page for the 3rd-place match shows the veto as it was posted before the semifinal loser was even decided, and its own summary text still says "Aurora's opponent is still to be decided" after Falcons qualified. Anything on this site that prices a specific map veto is guessing.
