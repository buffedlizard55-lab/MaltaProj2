How the contrarian rule was set, using this event as the test

Day 1 was the lesson: Aurora, not 9z, were the favorite that fell - HLTV wrote that they were "the only favorite to falter in Malta" after losing 2-0 to 9z (13-11 Inferno, 13-1 Nuke). Day 4 gave the second shock: BETBOOM beat Falcons 2-1 (Anubis 13-5, Dust2 14-16, Mirage 7-13) and HotSpawn's own event report called them "Huge underdogs coming into it". Both upsets were a favorite losing at a LAN, not a 30% dog being cheap. So this desk does not fade the crowd reflexively; it fades the crowd only where a priced line says the dog is at least 35% live. Applied to day 5 that means Aurora at 39-40% is playable for variance and BETBOOM at ~31% is not, even though BETBOOM is the team that just knocked out the number-one seed.
