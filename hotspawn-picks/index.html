How a pick is made (30-second version)

  1. Featured ★ — de-vigged consensus of primary bookmakers, copied unchanged. Never overwritten by fallback research.
  2. HLTV — fixture/roster/schedule identity only (no odds feed; bot probe 403 recorded).
  3. Esportsbooks/exchange — Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Thunderpick, Kalshi quotes joined on both teams + kickoff (±15 min); alias joins only with 5/5 roster evidence.
  4. Valve VRS model — official points → Glicko curve with market-calibrated slope k=0.4308 (fit on 60 priced pairs).
  5. † Floor / blank — one team unrated → directional bound; neither priced nor rated → blank, never invented.
