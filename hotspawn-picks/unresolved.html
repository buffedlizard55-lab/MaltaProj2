Standing methodological limitations

  • Unrated teams: Valve VRS requires counted matches with a stable Core; new stacks (TitanFlow, Off Chops, Staqued, LOS NINOS) have no official points. Floor bounds assume 'no stronger than the weakest rated team' only for direction.
  • Duplicate VRS names: the same org appears with old/new Cores (Banda Chuya #165 vs #373; Betclic #45 vs #183). Only the active 5-player Core with the latest counted match (or a roster-verified Core) is used; ambiguous pairs stay blank.
  • Thin exchanges: Kalshi books with >10¢ spreads and ~$5-volume Polymarket markets are rejected as prices. A mid-price from a crossed/empty book is not a probability.
  • Snapshot decay: odds expire at lock or after ~4–8h. The 4-hourly bot re-runs the whole ladder; model rows upgrade to market rows automatically when books post.
  • HLTV access: automated HLTV reads are 403-blocked; HLTV is used via dated manual event/team reads for identity, which do not scale to every future fixture — new sponsor aliases may be missed until flagged.
