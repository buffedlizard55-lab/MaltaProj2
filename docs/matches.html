"bc.game line (via EGW)" is the price shown in EGamersWorld's Bets on <event> by bc.game module, converted to a de-margined probability. "Our line (consensus)" is the equal-weight average of the available de-margined sources (bc.game via EGamersWorld and/or Polymarket). Rows without any captured external line are left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Kickoff times are UTC. They come from the lobby payload's machine-readable start timestamps (independent audit: 86-row evidence file) and match the HLTV match pages; the clock labels rendered on Hotspawn's own pages read 4 hours earlier (US Eastern), so a 17:00 label there is 21:00 UTC here. Every fixture also carries its Hotspawn, HLTV and market links in the last column.
