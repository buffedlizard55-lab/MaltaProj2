Method. Each probability is the proportional de-vig of a two-way external bookmaker market: p = (1/o) / (1/o1 + 1/o2), with decimal odds captured on 2026-10-13 from GG.BET, BC.GAME or N1 Bet bookmaker panels (via EGamersWorld event/match pages), plus GG.bet (tips.gg/win.gg) and Polymarket cross-checks. Hotspawn community pick percentages are displayed for context only and are never used as probability indicators. Matches with no published market stay blank and are flagged unverified — nothing is inferred or filled.
