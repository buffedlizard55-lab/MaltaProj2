Methodology

Explicit inputs. Reproducible arithmetic. Missing evidence stays missing.

01 / Discover, do not guess

Read the official public lobby HTML, then its script#ng-state transfer-state. Locate the response whose URL ends in /api/v1/fixtures; keys are discovered rather than hard-coded. Read the embedded permalink map without calling its admin-named endpoint. Preserve fixture IDs and source epoch timestamps. Inspect rendered cards separately from embedded records.

02 / Verify a market before joining it

A valid current join needs both participant identities, competition, UTC start, series format, full Match Winner market, outcome coverage, quote timestamp and source URL. Similar team names or dates alone do not qualify. There are currently no validated joins. No authentication or anti-bot bypass is attempted.

03 / Originate a reference line

For American odds a ≥ +100: decimal d = 1 + a/100. For a ≤ −100: d = 1 + 100/|a|. For each outcome q = 1/d. Overround = qA + qB − 1. Our proportional no-vig estimate pA = qA/(qA + qB), pB = qB/(qA + qB). Our fair decimal lines are 1/pA and 1/pB.

This is a disclosed modeling choice, not a sourced fact or a claim of true probability. Do not apply a two-outcome calculation to a three-way market, map handicap, partial market, or mixed live/pre-match quotes.

Odds-conversion formulas were also checked against the official Smarkets help article. Proportional margin removal remains our modeling choice.

04 / Refresh without misrepresenting freshness

GitHub Actions attempts public-source collection hourly. The collector retains the previous snapshot on failures and publishes a source-health report. HTTP Date, cache Age, retrieval time and snapshot hash remain visible. A new build is not a new odds observation. Reference quotes retain their original observed date; no automatic odds adapter is validated.

05 / Limits

We do not assign neutral 50/50 probabilities, extrapolate missing odds, interpret pick popularity as strength, or fit an unsupported statistical model. Historical scheduled records remain visible and flagged. A complete up-to-date priced list has not been achieved.
