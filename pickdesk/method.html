Method · verification · cadence

How every number on this desk is produced

1. Fixture discovery (never probability)

Fixtures come from the public HotSpawn lobby page and HotSpawn's own fixtures endpoint, swept per competition id seen in the lobby transfer state plus the ids HotSpawn publishes in its own links (competition 35859 carries the TWC semi-finals). A fixture is open while its lock time has not passed. Crowd pick percentages are stored as crowd_* ownership and are read by nothing except the differential formula below.

2. Prices (external only)

Pinnacle guest odds, Bovada coupons, the Melbet/22bet/Betwinner LineFeed, the Kalshi exchange, Thunderpick coefficients published per fixture by bo3.gg, and Polymarket with a traded book. Sources that refuse the runner (HLTV, EGamersWorld, GG.bet, Stake, Betano, VBet, Thunderpick's own site — HTTP 403) are recorded as refusals and read by hand with a dated citation.

3. Arithmetic

  • Decimal → implied probability: p = 1 / decimal.
  • No-vig two-way split: p_a = (1/d_a) / (1/d_a + 1/d_b); the overround is published per quote.
  • Consensus: weighted mean of the accepted sources' no-vig splits; weights below.
  • Originated line (only when no market prices the fixture): p = 1 / (1 + 10^(-Δpoints/400)) applied to Valve's official regional standings, always labelled ORIGINATED.
  • Expected leaderboard differential: points × (probability − crowd ownership).

4. Gates

  • Kalshi books with a spread above 0.1 are refused as thin.
  • Polymarket books below $25 traded volume are refused as untraded.
  • Climb (contrarian) pick: at least 0.2 market probability, a positive market-vs-crowd differential, at least two verified context flags, and at most 3 activations per day.

5. Cadence and automation

.github/workflows/pickdesk-34af18bf.yml re-collects, re-derives and re-renders this section every four hours and on demand; collection and rendering run in a GitHub-hosted runner with general internet access. No step needs manual input, and a failing verification step blocks the commit so the previous good build stays published.

6. Verification performed

  • Every published probability is re-derived from the retained raw quotes in data/34af18bf/snapshot.json by scripts/34af18bf/verify.py; a mismatch fails the build.
  • Every row must carry at least one official/external source link; rows without one fail the build.
  • No HotSpawn crowd field may appear in any arithmetic input (asserted in code).
  • Internal links are checked before publish; the same check runs again in scripts/stage_site.py.
