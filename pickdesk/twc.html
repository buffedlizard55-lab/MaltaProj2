Why these picks — contrarian but not stupid

The leaderboard is scored on pick'em points, so the value of a pick is not "who wins" but points × (true probability − crowd ownership). A pick everybody owns is worth nothing when it lands and costs you the week when it does not. The gates below are published so you can check every activation.

RuleGate
C1Not stupid: a climb pick must keep at least a 20% win probability from the external market consensus — never below one win in five. Below that it is a lottery ticket, not a pick. The market must also be the one that gives it that chance: a crowd-only underdog with no book price is not eligible.
C2Real differential: the pick's market probability must exceed its HotSpawn crowd ownership share. Expected differential = offered points × (probability − ownership).
C3Two verified context flags before a climb pick is activated: a head-to-head result, a roster/onboarding change, a rest/back-to-back edge, or a veto/map-pool edge — each with its own dated source.
C4Maximum three activated climb picks per day; every other match gets the market favourite as the safe pick.
C5A crowd percentage is ownership only. It never enters a probability, and HotSpawn is never used as a source of win probability.
