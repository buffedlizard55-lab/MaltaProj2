Standing limitations of this desk

  • A pick is a model output from market prices, not a guarantee. Rows whose only basis is the originated Valve-VRS transform are labelled ORIGINATED on every page.
  • Where no external two-sided price exists and the ratings are missing or ambiguous, the probability stays blank and the row appears in the Unresolved picks & limitations section — never as an invented 50/50.
