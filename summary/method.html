Method

Where every number comes from, and where the model stops.

Where the match list comes from

The public HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby embeds its fixture list in the page. That list is the inventory. HotSpawn is used for fixture discovery only; its crowd “picked %” is never used as a probability, so our numbers are independent of what other players picked.

Where the probabilities come from

  1. Bookmaker and exchange prices. Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet and Kalshi full-match prices, read by the OURLINE collector. Each side's implied probability is de-vigged proportionally (p = (1/odds) / sum(1/odds)) and the sources are averaged. Every priced row links to the bookmaker page it came from.
  2. Rating model (when there is no price). Valve's own published standings give every team a points total. Valve's repository documents the win-expectancy curve (Q = ln(10)/400, g = 1/sqrt(1 + 3*Q^2*RD^2/pi^2), RD fixed at 75), so P(A beats B) = sigmoid(k · g · ln(10)/400 · (ptsA - ptsB)). Valve's README warns their curve is too steep, so k is not assumed: it is fitted by least squares against observed bookmaker lines. The value used here is the one the OURLINE board published, k = 0.3590, fitted on 12 market/model pairs with a mean absolute error of 0.0829.
  3. Blend. Where both exist, market and model are blended in logit space with the market weighted 0.85 when two or more independent sources agree, 0.75 otherwise. That weighting and the blend are the OURLINE board's published choices; this section does not change them.

Name resolution

Some teams appear under different names at HotSpawn, Valve and HLTV. Resolutions are only made when two independent official sources agree through the same event, the same opponents and the same kickoff offsets, and every one is published with its evidence on the Uncovered matches page.

Limits

  • A rating-model pick is our own estimate, not a bookmaker line. No bookmaker is quoting these matches, which is exactly why they were uncovered.
  • Valve's standings are a snapshot (2026_10_05); rosters and ratings move between snapshots.
  • Where a team has no official rating at all in the sources we could read, the row stays blank and is flagged. It is never filled in.
  • Probabilities are estimates, not predictions with any validated accuracy, and not betting advice.
