Method & sources

How each pick is resolved

  1. Layer M (market): a verified external bookmaker/exchange price (Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Kalshi, or the Thunderpick coefficients bo3.gg publishes per fixture) with confirmed participant alignment resolves the pick, exactly like the featured picks. Research is not used to override a market pick.
  2. Layer R (research): no external price exists -> read HLTV (ranking, analytics page, head-to-head, form, roster/stand-in notes), then esportsbooks / esports betting-line aggregators (egamersworld odds widget, Polymarket listings, bo3.gg analytics, Kalshi event pages). An own line is originated from those documented facts; each number on the site links to its source page.
  3. Layer O (model): no market and no usable research -> the project's Valve VRS / own-Elo originated line, labelled as such.
  4. Layer U (unresolved): nothing defensible -> pick left blank and published on the Unresolved picks + limitations page. Never inferred, never filled in.

Own originated line per match = a documented probability derived from the research facts listed per fixture (HLTV rank gap, head-to-head, recent form, roster notes, any published bookmaker/aggregator quote with its no-vig normalization p_i = (1/odds_i) / sum_j(1/odds_j)). Each originated probability is stated explicitly below with the facts it uses. Estimates only — not guarantees.

Probability derivation

  • No-vig from a two-way price: p_i = (1/odds_i) / sum_j(1/odds_j) (proportional method).
  • Market consensus: arithmetic mean of per-source no-vig probabilities (as on the full-coverage board).
  • Research-resolved rows: the originated probability is stated per fixture in data/b5436cf0/research.json with the exact facts used.
  • HotSpawn crowd pick percentages are never used as probability inputs.
