Limitations of generating picks for low-tier matches

  • Featured market resolution (Pinnacle/Bovada/Melbet full-match moneylines) rarely covers tier-B/C CS2; those rows depend on thinner sources (Kalshi exchange books, Thunderpick coefficients published via bo3.gg) or research.
  • HLTV returns HTTP 403 to GitHub Actions runners; HLTV facts are session-researched with dated read times and re-verified by later research passes rather than re-read on every 4-hour refresh.
  • egamersworld often shows an undifferentiated 1.8 / 1.8 even widget price for unpriced matches — it is recorded but never used as a line.
  • Prediction markets (Polymarket) list some of these matches with ~$0 volume; placeholder pricing is never used.
  • Bo1 LAN group-stage matches (Elisa BreakOut Series 2) carry higher single-map variance; confidence labels are capped accordingly.
  • Every probability on this site is an estimate derived from the linked sources at their read times. Odds move; matches get rescheduled; rosters change. Not betting advice.
