📖 Method & sources

How a probability is produced

For decimal odds dA, dB: fair pA = (1/dA) / ((1/dA)+(1/dB)) (bookmaker margin removed). Exchange cents are normalised the same way. Multiple accepted sources are averaged as declared per row. Worked example (SF1, read 2026-10-17T01:05:00Z): egamersworld 1.83/1.90 → FURIA 51%; Polymarket 53¢/48¢ → FURIA 52%; mean → FURIA 52% / Aurora 48%.

Contrarian rule

A "climb" pick is only shown when the underdog's fair probability is ≥40%. Below that, fading the favourite loses more expected points than differentiation can repay — the definition of "stupid" used across this site.
