Method & sources

📖 How every number is made

Probability from a market

For decimal odds dA, dB: pA = (1/dA) / ((1/dA) + (1/dB)). The overround is removed. Exchange cents are normalised the same way. Accepted two-sided sources are averaged with equal weight and the row lists them all.

Probability from official ratings

pA = 1 / (1 + 10^(-(PtsA - PtsB) × k / 400)), with Pts from Valve’s published regional standings and k = 0.421341 fitted on 11 market/model pairs (MAE 0.075). The 400-point scale is a stated modelling assumption, not a Valve-published quantity.

What is never used

  • HotSpawn “picked %” — it is crowd ownership, shown only as context.
  • The egamersworld 1.80/1.80 default — a placeholder, rejected.
  • A forced 50/50 to fill a blank row.
