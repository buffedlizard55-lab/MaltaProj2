The resolution ladder (applied in this order)

  1. 1. Two-sided bookmaker line from a primary book (Pinnacle / Bovada / Melbet) via the canonical 4-hourly collector.
  2. 2. Two-sided line from a secondary book or exchange (stavka-quoted books, kushvsporte, vprognoze, egamersworld bookmaker average, Polymarket).
  3. 3. HLTV betting-analytics page - checked for every unpriced fixture; HLTV lists no book price for any of the four unpriced matches this week.
  4. 4. Originated model from the official Valve VRS pair (both rosters uniquely identified, standings <= 14 days old).
  5. 5. Direction-only pick with a blank percentage, flagged low-confidence, when one side has no official rating.
  6. 6. Nothing else. A 1.80/1.80 placeholder is rejected rather than republished.

Known limitations

  • Direction-only rows. A pick without a percentage is a judgement from ranking, form and head-to-head, not a priced edge. Treat it as low confidence.
  • Model rows. The originated line uses official Valve VRS points and a 400-point logistic scale fitted on 11 market pairs (MAE 0.075). The scale is this project's assumption, not a Valve quantity.
  • Alias joins. ex-6666 → Valve’s “6666”, WROTBERRY → WBT / WhiteBIT Team. Both are documented on the row and flagged.
  • Single-book rows. Lavked–LFO 10 rests on one two-sided book; re-check before lock.
  • Line movement. Every probability carries its capture time. A price read at 09:00 UTC is not the price at lock.
  • Inventory lag. This page rebuilds from the canonical 4-hourly collector. A fixture added to HotSpawn in the last few hours may not appear until the next run.
