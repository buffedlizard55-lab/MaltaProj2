The resolution ladder (applied in order)

  1. Esportsbooks first: Pinnacle / Bovada / Melbet full-match two-way prices, both teams + kickoff joined (canonical collector).
  2. HLTV match pages for identity/schedule (HLTV publishes no directly reusable odds feed; probe results are retained).
  3. Exchanges / other books: Polymarket / Kalshi / egamersworld-listed books where a full-match market exists (day-4 desk).
  4. Originated line: Valve official VRS standings → Glicko logistic model, only when both rosters are uniquely identified and standings ≤14 days old. Labelled "model", never presented as a bookmaker line.
  5. Otherwise blank and listed here with the failure reason.

Limitations you should know

  • Low-tier (B/C) matches are often priced by no book and absent from Polymarket — the blank is a market-coverage fact, not laziness.
  • Team-name aliases (academy/ex- rosters, slot names) block the VRS model when identity can't be proven; we never borrow a sibling roster's rating.
  • Lines move: every probability carries its source + capture time in board.json; re-check before lock.
  • HotSpawn crowd % is shown nowhere as a probability — it measures other players' picks, not win chances.
