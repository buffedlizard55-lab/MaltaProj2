Method & verification

Sources, formulas, join rules, calibration of the originated models and the limitations of this board.

Two rules drive every number on this site. (1) A probability is only published when an external, non-HotSpawn source confirms it — or when a documented model covers both teams, in which case the row says “originated estimate”. (2) If neither holds, the value stays blank and the row is published on Flagged for review. Values are never inferred or filled in.

Formulas

StepRule
Implied probabilityraw = 1 / decimal odds for each side
Overroundraw_a + raw_b
No-vigp_a = raw_a / overround, p_b = raw_b / overround (proportional method)
Consensusarithmetic mean of the per-source no-vig probabilities (bookmakers only; the exchange is never averaged in)
Valve standings estimatep = 1 / (1 + 10^(−(points_a − points_b)/400)); the 400-point scale is a stated modelling assumption, not a Valve-published number
Fitted modelBradley-Terry (logistic) on finished bo3.gg matches: p = 1 / (1 + exp(−(r_a − r_b))), half-life 60.0 days, L2 ridge 0.02, 600 gradient steps, a team is only used with at least 8 matches in the corpus
Pickthe higher of the two published probabilities; an edge below 0.005 is published as a coin flip with no pick
Join ruleboth team labels must match after canonicalisation (case, accents, punctuation, leading “team”/“the”, trailing “esports”/“esport”/“gaming”/“team” only), and any published start time must be within ±240 minutes
Freshnessmarket quotes older than 6 hours and in-play prices are excluded and listed as refused candidates

Does the originated model add value? (calibration)

Measured on finished CS2 matches in the last 14 days of the bo3.gg corpus. The Bradley-Terry model is refitted with that whole window excluded (out-of-sample) and the Valve snapshot predates the window. The book price pair bo3.gg keeps for a finished match is its last (in-play) price and leaks the result, so it is not scored here as a benchmark. Reported as Brier score and log loss (lower is better; a coin flip has a Brier score of 0.25).

Limitations

  • Bookmaker coverage of tier-3 matches is genuinely thin; where no book prices a match the row stays blank rather than borrowing another match's price or a crowd percentage.
  • Start times are those HotSpawn publishes (UTC, from the lobby API). Where a source publishes a different time the difference is flagged on the row and the same price is listed as a refused candidate if it exceeds ±240 minutes.
  • An exchange mid price is not a bookmaker quote: it is published with its spread and never averaged into a bookmaker consensus.
  • Originated estimates are model output, clearly labelled, and are not guarantees or official forecasts. The calibration table is an out-of-sample measurement on recent matches, not a promise of future accuracy.
  • HotSpawn's crowd “picked %” is displayed for context only and is never a probability input.
