Method & sources

What is measured, what is modelled, and what this board refuses to claim.

1 · Fixture discovery (HotSpawn, reverse engineered)

The public lobby page ships an Angular transfer state (script#ng-state) containing the same /api/v1/fixtures response the page renders, plus the permalink map HotSpawn itself publishes for each match. This board reads that public state and the public fixtures API — no admin endpoint, no authentication, no scraping of the DOM. Only CS2 rows are kept. Crowd "picked %" values are refused at the parser (core.FIXTURE_KEYS) and cannot reach a probability.

2 · Market prices (external, never HotSpawn)

  • Pinnacle — licensed bookmaker, public guest odds API (moneyline, period 0, not alternate, status open).
  • Bovada — licensed bookmaker, public coupon JSON (moneyline, main period).
  • Melbet / 22bet / Betwinner — licensed bookmakers on the 1xBet platform (mirror family; one quote per event).
  • Kalshi — CFTC-regulated exchange, series KXCS2GAME; mid of the bid/ask, and books wider than 10 cents or one-sided are rejected.
  • Thunderpick — licensed esports bookmaker; its two-way coefficients are read from the public bet_updates block of the bo3.gg API, which is what covers the tier-C events no other reachable bookmaker lists. The row links to the bo3.gg match page.

Margin removal is proportional: p = (1/odds) / Σ(1/odds). The consensus is a source-weighted mean (Pinnacle 2.0, Bovada 1.5, Thunderpick 1.5, Kalshi 1.0, Melbet 1.0) and the min–max spread is published on the row when two or more sources quote it.

3 · Originated lines (this project's own numbers)

Where no reachable price exists, the board originates a line instead of leaving the match blank, and labels it:

  • Valve VRS model. Valve's own published relationship applies: a 400-point rating difference equals ~90% win probability. With C = g·ln(10)/400, g = 1/√(1+3Q²RD²/π²), Q = ln(10)/400, RD = 75 (Valve's model/glicko.js and defaultGlickoParams), the model probability is σ(k·C·Δpoints). The slope k is fitted from this build's own market/VRS pairs (k = 0.42016661039654146 over 9 pairs; Valve's own curve would be k=1) because Valve's README states the published curve is too steep at the ends.
  • Our Elo (last resort, directional). When at least one team has no VRS row, an Elo is computed from the finished matches bo3.gg publishes (result and score only — a factual record, not a price): 1500 + (VRS points − anchor) seeds teams that do have a VRS row; a team Valve does not list at all starts at the lowest points value Valve publishes in the snapshot (381.0 pts → Elo 1018.0) rather than at the median, and is then moved by its own results; 8 passes, K=20. This build used 692 finished matches and seeded 102 teams. Rows built this way are labelled directional estimate and listed on Unresolved picks & limitations.

Ratings used: Valve regional standings snapshot 2026_10_05 (americas (83 teams), asia (43 teams), europe (224 teams), global (349 teams)), fetched from the official ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings repository.

4 · Joining rules (fail closed)

  • Both team names must match after normalisation (case/accents/punctuation folded; a leading "Team/The" and a trailing "Team/Esports/Gaming/GG/Club" dropped) — or a published alias must apply.
  • "ex-" roster labels (HLTV/HotSpawn "ex-Org" vs a bookmaker's "Org") join only when the opponent is identical and both sources state the same kick-off within 15 minutes; the join is flagged with its evidence and is never used to borrow the organisation's Valve rating.
  • The source's own start time must be within 14 hours of the lobby kickoff; differences over 3 hours are flagged on the row.
  • Exactly one price per fixture and source; the first qualifying moneyline is used.
  • A quote that does not join is published as unmatched rather than forced onto a fixture.

5 · Limits of this board

  • Originated lines are model output, not market prices; they are labelled on every row and counted separately in the executive summary.
  • Sources can be blocked from the collector's network. Every failure is published on the flagged page; a blocked source never silently becomes a blank probability.
  • Kalshi kickoff times are encoded in US Eastern time in the event ticker and are used only for the join window — never displayed as a kickoff.
  • This is not betting advice, not a guarantee, and not a claim of validated predictive accuracy; the track record page reports the realised hit rate as it accumulates.

6 · Reproduce it

python3 scripts/fe803b80/collect.py writes data/fe803b80/snapshot.json; python3 scripts/fe803b80/build.py re-derives every number on this site from that snapshot and writes data/fe803b80/board.json; python3 scripts/fe803b80/site.py renders these pages. python3 -m unittest tests.test_fe803b80 -v re-checks the rules above offline.
