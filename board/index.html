How to read a pick

  • PICK the team with the higher final probability on that row.
  • market the number comes from external prices only (bookmakers/exchange), margin removed with the proportional no-vig method.
  • market+model external prices blended with the official Valve VRS rating model (logit blend, market weight 0.85 with 2+ sources, 0.75 with one).
  • originated no reachable bookmaker prices this fixture, so the line is originated here: official Valve VRS points, or — when the teams have no VRS row — this project's own Elo built from published bo3.gg match results. Labelled, never mixed silently.
  • unverified nothing sourced could support a number; the cell stays blank and the row is on the flagged page.
