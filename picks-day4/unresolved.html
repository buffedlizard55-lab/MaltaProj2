8. Team names we could not verify against Valve's official standings

These names appear on HotSpawn fixtures but not in Valve's Regional Standings snapshot, and no verified alias exists for them. They are left unresolved on purpose: guessing a mapping would put a fake number in front of a real decision.

  • ex-6666 — Valve's snapshot contains two different rosters both named "6666" (global #127 amster/dENZY/kL1o/rendysky/skcH and #368 auth0ri/m4d4ra/rickangel/skcH), so the mapping is ambiguous.
  • Apogee Betclic, Insiders, Morningstar, WROTBERRY, WXM, antidaun — not listed in the snapshot at all (regional files included). Valve standings repository

Consequence: any fixture containing one of these sides can only be priced from a market quote. If no market joined, the row stays blank and appears in section 1.

9. Known coverage limits of this desk (stated plainly)

  • Pinnacle's esports API and egamersworld were not scraped by the automated collector; where they carry a line this desk may be missing the sharpest price available. Recorded as a limitation, not silently ignored.
  • HLTV publishes match pages and analytics text but no book-by-book odds table on the analytics pages read this session, so HLTV is used for results, rosters, ratings and veto tendencies — not for prices.
  • HotSpawn's streak bonus value is not published anywhere, so it cannot be optimised; it is used only as a tie-break (rule R5) and flagged.
  • The Valve standings snapshot is dated 2026_10_05; a roster move after that date is invisible to the originated model line.
  • Polymarket coverage of tier-B/C CS2 is sparse: most lobby fixtures are priced from bookmaker lines only.
