The strategy in three sentences

HotSpawn pays a flat number of points per correct pick (tier multiplies the points, ownership does not) plus an unpublished streak bonus, so expected points = offered_points × P(win) — the leaderboard-optimal play is simply the highest-probability side of a correct line, and "contrarian" only earns anything when it is free. It is free when our line's favourite is not the crowd's favourite (rule R2), and it is defensible at sub-50% only with two or more independently verified context facts and real points on the table (rule R3, capped at two per day). Everything else is variance, and variance is labelled as variance rather than sold as an edge.

Sources: HotSpawn how it works (official scoring) · HotSpawn fixtures API row 1081888 (points_breakdown has no ownership term)

Why fading the crowd was right on day 1 — and how often it works

9z beat Aurora 2-0 on day 1 with the crowd 93% on Aurora: the 7% who took 9z collected the same flat 7 points as everyone who got a favourite right. Across the eleven settled TWC fixtures, the nine with a ≥60% crowd favourite went 6-3 — right often enough to respect, wrong often enough to fade when our own line disagrees. The three failures were Aurora at 93% (v 9z), PARIVISION at 64% (v BETBOOM) and Legacy at 88% (v BETBOOM); the winners were held by 7%, 36% and 12% of pickers.

Where every number comes from

  • Fixtures, points, crowd ownership, settlement: HotSpawn fixtures API — discovery and settlement only, never a probability input.
  • Prices: Thunderpick coefficients as published by bo3.gg, Melbet's public esports LineFeed, and Polymarket exchange prices (volume-weighted). All converted to no-vig probabilities.
  • Our originated line: logistic on the official Valve Regional Standings point delta (Valve repository), scale fitted to the markets captured in the same run — 76 pairs, R² 0.4522, mean absolute error 0.082.
  • Editorial facts (results, map scores, ratings, roster history, rules): HLTV, Valve, HotSpawn's own pages and Polymarket, each cited where it is used.
