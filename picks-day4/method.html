Hard rule: HotSpawn is used for fixture discovery, tier, offered points, lock times, crowd ownership and settlement — and never as a probability indicator. Crowd ownership is always labelled as crowd ownership.

Join rule (how a price is attached to a fixture)

A quote is attached to a HotSpawn fixture only when both hold:

  • its start time is within 90 minutes of the fixture's scheduled_start_time (Melbet and bo3.gg matched HotSpawn to the second on the fixtures checked);
  • at least one side matches by team name (exact or substring), and the other side does not contradict it.

Rejected quotes are published in full on Unresolved — including the Melbet row that names the same two teams 2.5 hours off HotSpawn's start time. Joins that are not exact on both names carry a "partial" confidence label on the row itself.

No-vig conversion

Bookmaker decimal odds are converted with p_a = (1/o_a) / ((1/o_a) + (1/o_b)), which removes the overround rather than trusting either side's raw price. Polymarket prices are already probabilities and sum to ~1.0; they are renormalised to remove rounding only. The overround actually observed on each quote is printed on the row.

Consensus weighting

Books (Thunderpick via bo3.gg, Melbet) are weighted 1.0. Polymarket is weighted 0.5–1.25 by traded volume (0.5 + log10(volume)/4, capped) because an exchange price carries no vig but a thin book is noisy. The consensus is the weighted average of the no-vig probabilities; if the sources disagree by more than 12 points the row is flagged.

Our originated line (Valve VRS logistic)

p_a = 1 / (1 + exp(−Δ/S)) where Δ is the point difference in Valve's official Regional Standings and S is fitted, not asserted: least squares of logit(market p_a) on Δ through the origin, over every quote in this capture whose two sides both resolve in the standings.

Which number the pick uses

  • ≥2 market sources → consensus.
  • 1 market source → 75% consensus + 25% originated VRS line (shrinks a thin quote).
  • No market → originated VRS line alone, labelled "our model only" and listed as a limitation.
  • Neither → blank, UNVERIFIED, on Unresolved. Never guessed.

Decision rules

R1 — Maximum-probability pick (default) gate: always applies · cost: none

Because points are flat per correct pick, expected points = offered_points x P(win). The leaderboard-optimal pick is always the higher-probability side of our independent line. Fading the crowd earns no bonus.

R2 — Free contrarian (information edge) gate: our pick probability >= 50% AND crowd ownership of our pick <= 40% · cost: none versus R1

Where our line's favourite is NOT the crowd's favourite, R1 and contrarianism point the same way: take the higher-probability side and collect the ownership differential for free. These are the only contrarian picks this desk calls 'climb' picks by default.

R3 — Verified-context swing (labelled, capped) gate: our probability >= 30% (the 'not stupid' floor) AND >= 2 verified context flags AND offered decimal >= 1/our probability · cost: negative EV versus R1; variance only

A sub-50% side may be taken only with two or more independently verified context flags (roster change / onboarding, head-to-head inside 30 days, rest or schedule, in-event form swing) AND a market price at or above the break-even decimal for our own fair probability. Maximum two per day, and only on fixtures worth 3 points or more.

R4 — Variance play when behind (explicit, not recommended by default) gate: user is behind the prize cut with few picks left · cost: negative EV; explicit variance

Hotspawn's prizes go to the top 10 and the board resets weekly. A player trailing the 10th place score late in the week can only close the gap with variance, so a small number of R3 swings is rational for that player and irrational for the leader. The gap and the number of picks left are user-side inputs we do not have, so this rule is published as a decision aid, never auto-applied.

R5 — Streak protection gate: tie-break only · cost: unquantified — flagged

HotSpawn awards bonus points for consecutive correct picks but does not publish the bonus value, so it cannot be optimised. Until it is quantified the desk treats it as a tie-breaker only: between two picks of equal probability, prefer the one that keeps an existing streak alive (the higher-probability side).

HotSpawn scoring as officially published

  • Picks lock 5 minutes before the scheduled start time (also published per fixture by the API: lock_offset_sec = 360).
  • 'Earn points for every correct pick. Higher tier matches reward more points.' No ownership-scaled payout is published.
  • 'Get bonus points for consecutive correct picks. The longer your streak, the bigger the bonus.' The bonus value is not published.
  • Forfeited match: all predictions voided, no points awarded or deducted.
  • Cancelled match: all predictions cancelled, no points, excluded from the leaderboard.
  • The leaderboard resets every week.

Refresh cadence

The workflow day4live-17866d06.yml runs collect → model → build → tests → stage every 4 hours and on demand, commits the evidence and the rendered pages to this branch, and triggers the Pages deployment. That satisfies the 4–8 hour re-check requirement: HotSpawn adds fixtures continuously, and each run re-reads the whole scheduled list rather than a cached subset.

If a run fails, the previously committed pages stay live — a failed refresh never blanks the site.

Verification protocol

  • Every table row carries a link to the source that produced it (official site, bookmaker feed, exchange API or Valve's repository). No row is published without one.
  • A value that could not be confirmed is rendered blank with an UNVERIFIED tag. It is never inferred, averaged from a neighbour, or copied from another board.
  • Editorial claims (results, map scores, ratings, roster moves) are taken only from sources read this session and stored verbatim in scripts/17866d06/research.py with their URLs.
  • Raw responses are committed under data/17866d06/evidence/ with byte counts and SHA-256 digests in collect_log.json, so any published number can be replayed from stored evidence.
  • Crowd percentages are recorded verbatim even when a pair sums to more than 100% (observed on the lobby), because renormalising them would invent data.
