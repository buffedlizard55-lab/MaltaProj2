The day-1 upset, and what it teaches about the crowd

Aurora went into day 1 at 93% crowd ownership against 9z and lost 0-2 (Anubis 9-13, Mirage 11-13). The 7% who backed 9z were paid the same flat 7 points as anyone who backed a favourite. That is the whole contrarian case in one row: HotSpawn does not pay more for being alone, so the only reason to fade a crowd is that you believe the crowd is wrong — and the only defensible basis for believing that is a line of your own plus verified context, not a feeling.

Across all eleven settled fixtures the nine ≥60% crowd favourites went 0-0. Fading blindly would have lost points six times. Fading only where our originated line disagrees with the crowd is the play.
