Formulas

  • De-vig: p = (1/odds) ÷ (1/oddsA + 1/oddsB).
  • Several books: weighted mean of de-vigged probabilities (Pinnacle 2, Bovada 1.5, Thunderpick 1.5, Kalshi 1, Melbet 1).
  • Blend with ratings: logit blend, market weight 0.85 with two or more books, 0.75 with one (TWC uses one book).
  • Valve model: p = 1/(1+e−k·c·Δpoints), c from Valve's own Glicko code, slope k fitted to published market pairs (board k = 0.25; TWC k = 0.40188 fitted to 48 pairs).
  • Pick: the side with the higher probability. If only one side has any rating or result history: that side, probability blank.
  • Climb rule: keep the pick unless the other side is ≥40% on our line, ≤25% of the crowd and beats its crowd share by ≥15 pts, with at least one book price behind it. Contrarian is only worth it when it is close to a coin-flip.
  • TWC credible upset: an underdog at or above 35% on the de-vigged book price.

Refresh

The line board refreshes about every 30 minutes (workflow board (fe803b80)); this front door rebuilds every 4 hours and whenever the board workflow finishes, re-reading HotSpawn's crowd split. A banner appears if the lines are more than 8 hours old. Matches past their lock time move to the "Locked" table automatically.

HotSpawn lobby, reverse-engineered

The lobby is an Angular page. Its public fixtures API (/api/v1/fixtures) lists every fixture with status, kickoff, lock time (6 minutes before kickoff), points and per-side picked_percent; the page's embedded state carries the same list. We use it for discovery, locks, results and the crowd split only.
