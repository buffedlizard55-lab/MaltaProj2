Standing limitations of this hub

  • HotSpawn is used for fixture discovery, listed points/tier, lock times and crowd ownership only. It is never a probability input.
  • No source reviewed here publishes how many days of rest a team had, so no rest adjustment is applied anywhere.
  • The model column is this project's own calibrated calculation from official Valve ratings. It is not a bookmaker line and carries no claimed out-of-sample accuracy.
  • Playoff fixtures beyond the two day-3 deciders were not listed on the HotSpawn lobby at capture time, so they are not priced here.
  • Ratings come from the 2026-10-05 global standings because that is the newest official global file published at capture time; HLTV's live VRS chip shows #5 for Aurora, which the model does not use.
