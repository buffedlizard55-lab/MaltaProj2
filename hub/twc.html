  • Aurora reached the IEM Cologne Major semi-final before the rebuild; after it they took 2nd at StarLadder StarSeries Fall 2026 (losing the final 1-3 to Vitality) and 3rd at ESL Pro League Season 24, and entered TWC 2026 as HLTV #6 / VRS #9 (5 October snapshot) source
  • Aurora's starting five at TWC 2026 is unchanged from the July rebuild: woxic, XANTARES, Wicadia, Jimpphat, kyxsan source
  • The opposing side of the decider, 9z, is the same five-man roster recorded in the official VRS on 2026-10-05 (dgt, HUASOPEEK, luchov, max, meyern) source

No source reviewed in this session states how many days off either roster had, or publishes any rest measurement, so no rest penalty or bonus is applied. The only verifiable onboarding facts are the dated July signings above and the 0.53 rating kyxsan posted in the day-1 loss at this event.

Day 3 — the desk

Safe pick = the side the eligible market or exchange line prices as favourite. Leaderboard swing = the side the crowd is fading (the underdog the market still prices, or a favourite the crowd has abandoned) and only when every published gate condition holds on the same capture; otherwise the row says why no swing was activated.

What would have to happen for the contrarian side to land

  • Aurora vs 9z. 9z beat Aurora 2-0 two days ago and owns Mirage, the map Aurora most often loses to teams of this tier; the contrarian side needs Aurora's Nuke/Anubis veto advantage to be neutralised in the ban phase and another big luchov series. Aurora's edge in the ratings and in HLTV's map statistics is real, which is why the gate still prices 9z as the underdog.
  • BetBoom vs Legacy. The crowd sits on Legacy, the market agrees (about 61%) but the rating model is closer to 73%, so the underdog is priced below the 30% floor of the gate on at least one reading. No swing is activated; the pick stays on the favourite with that disagreement published.
