Method, formulas and refresh schedule

Where each number comes from

  1. Fixtures, listed points, locks and crowd shares — the public HotSpawn lobby and its own embedded fixture response. The crowd share is ownership: it is shown next to the pick and is never used to derive a probability.
  2. Prices — licensed bookmaker two-way match-winner lines (Pinnacle weighted 2, Bovada and Melbet weighted 1) and public exchange books (Polymarket CLOB only when both sides of the book exist inside 10 cents and the kickoff matches within five minutes; the CFTC-regulated Kalshi KXCS2GAME series only through explicit full-match contract rules).
  3. Derivation — proportional no-vig: for decimal dA, dB, pA = (1/dA) / ((1/dA) + (1/dB)). Exchange readings use the midpoint of the live bid/ask book and are labelled as estimates, not executable prices.
  4. Own model line — only for rows with no eligible price, and only when both teams resolve to exactly one row of the official Valve regional standings. pA = 1 / (1 + 10^(-Δ·s·k)) where Δ is the points difference, s the rating scale and k the slope fitted on this capture's own bookmaker consensus: k=0.4223589195781866 (slope learned from this capture's own bookmaker consensus over official Valve ratings, through the origin, clamped to the published bounds), pairs used 6.
  5. Pick — the side with the higher derived probability; confidence labels are Strong ≥70%, Solid ≥60%, Lean ≥53%, otherwise Toss-up. Fair decimal = 1/p.

The contrarian (leaderboard) gate

  • description — Contrarian-but-not-stupid gate for leaderboard picks. All four conditions must hold on the same row and the same capture; the gate can and does select no candidate rather than force a fade.
  • G1_price_floor — The most conservative available market or exchange reading of the underdog must be at least 30%.
  • G2_crowd_gap — Crowd ownership of the underdog must be at least 15 percentage points below that reading, so the pick is genuinely contrarian rather than merely unpopular.
  • G3_dated_context — At least one dated, source-linked fact about the underdog (recent result, head-to-head, roster change, map/veto profile).
  • G4_model_sanity — If a rating-model estimate exists for the match, it must not sit more than 10 percentage points below the market reading of the underdog. This blocks fades that only the crowd disagrees with but both the market and the model reject.
  • points_differential — listed_points * (pick_probability - crowd_ownership_of_that_side), published in listed points only because the lobby never states how its tier and stage multipliers combine.

Differential = listed points × (probability − crowd ownership of that side), quoted in listed points only: the lobby advertises tier and stage multipliers but never states how they combine, so no score-adjusted number is invented.

Zero-blank ladder

  1. HLTV first (per-fixture pages and analytics; automated access is blocked and manual reads are dated)
  2. esportsbooks (Pinnacle guest API, Bovada, Melbet LineFeed) through the shared collector
  3. esports exchanges and betting lines (Polymarket CLOB markets, Kalshi KXCS2GAME series, rule-backed only)
  4. documented originated line from the official Valve regional standings, only when both teams resolve to exactly one non-ambiguous official row
  5. otherwise blank, with every attempt and its result published

A row that stops at the last step is published blank in the Unresolved section, never filled in.

Refresh

The hub rebuilds every four hours on a GitHub-hosted runner (cron 41 */4 * * *) and whenever the canonical collected batch changes.
