How to read a row (line-by-line verification)

Role: safe = expected-points pick (best available line); climb = leaderboard swing that passed every ladder gate (underdog fair ≥30%, crowd ownership ≤20%, probability/ownership ≥2×, ≥2 verified context flags); contrarian = against the market or the crowd with the gates documented; directional = pick without a publishable probability.
