Systemic limitations for low-tier / unpriced matches

  • No bookmaker price exists. Featured books (Pinnacle/Bovada/Melbet) and the Kalshi exchange price TWC and other S-tier series; tier-B/C CCT, Elisa and university rows are usually unpriced. Resolution order for those rows: HLTV facts → esportsbooks / betting lines (bo3.gg Thunderpick feed, esportbet, sportsgambler) → documented originated line from Valve VRS.
  • HLTV blocks datacenter IPs (403). HLTV facts are therefore session-researched and dated rather than re-read every refresh. Each is linked on its row.
  • Placeholder prices are rejected. egamersworld's uniform 1.80/1.80 micro-match quotes are treated as placeholders and never used; so are HLTV fan votes and HotSpawn crowd %.
  • Identity joins can be imperfect. Team aliases (e.g. WROTBERRY→WBT, RoundsGG→ROUNDS) were resolved by roster match against Valve's file, with residual risk documented in the picks-all flagged page.
  • Originated lines are estimates. The calibrated Valve-VRS curve has a 13-pt mean absolute error vs bookmaker lines; it is a fallback, not a verified probability.
  • Settlement lag. When a match locks before a line is captured (e.g. the day-2 TWC rows on some boards), that row can never gain a pick retroactively — it is listed as locked-with-no-line on the flagged page.
