How a pick is produced

  1. Real prices first. Two-way bookmaker and exchange prices are converted to no-vig probabilities (each book's two implied probabilities normalised to 100%), then averaged. Every book price, its decimal odds and its overround is printed on the row.
  2. Official ratings only when no book prices the match. Valve's published VRS points go through p = 1/(1+exp(-k·C·Δpoints)) with Valve's own C = 0.0056000078 and this project's fitted k = 0.4308 (re-fitted here: best k on a 60-pair grid is 0.448, MAE 7.70 pp).
  3. Missing rankings stay unresolved. Absence from Valve's table is not a strength bound. Without external prices or ratings for both teams, pick and probability cells stay blank.
  4. Nothing is guessed. 3 fixtures are unresolved. See the separate Unresolved picks & limitations list for evidence gaps.
Read this before betting a pick. Market rows are consensus prices; originated rows are model estimates, not official odds. The roughly 8 pp in-sample market-fit error is not a confidence interval or verified match-outcome accuracy. Details and the conflicting-signal rows are on Unresolved & limitations and Flagged for review.
