Method

How a pick and a probability are produced for every open HotSpawn CS2 match, in the order the sources are consulted, and what each label means.

1. Resolution ladder (first match wins)

StepSourceWhat it producesLabel
1Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet LineFeed, KalshiDe-vigged two-way prices, joined on both names + kickoff ±5 min; weighted mean (Pinnacle ×2)Verified
2Kalshi exchange order bookMid of bid/ask, both sides inside the 10¢ spread gate, kickoff from the exchange's own rules textVerified
3Official Valve Regional StandingsPoints difference through Valve's own Glicko curve with a fitted slope kOriginated
4Documented assumptionRated side only, published as a range between the VRS floor and the VRS median for the unrated opponentAssumption
5—Nothing: values are left blank and the reason is published Withheld

HotSpawn is discovery and settlement only. Its crowd pick percentage is never read, and no HotSpawn figure enters a probability.

2. De-vigging

For decimal prices d_a, d_b: i_a = 1/d_a, i_b = 1/d_b, p_a = i_a/(i_a+i_b). A book is rejected if its implied total is outside 0.95–1.30. American prices are converted first; Kalshi dollars are converted with d = 1/price. Every stored probability is re-derived from the raw prices at build time.

3. Valve rating model

Valve's own model (model/glicko.js, evaluated with a fixed RD of 75 as in model/fit.js) gives g = 0.9728, so p_A = 1/(1+10^(−k·g·Δ/400)). The slope k is fitted by least squares of logit(market) on g·Δ/400 over the retained calibration pairs: k = 0.4308 on 60 priced fixtures (unbounded fit 0.4308 if it fell outside the published bounds [0.25, 1.5]). Valve's README states its own curve overstates big gaps, which is why the slope is learned from bookmaker prices instead of assumed.

4. Assumption rule for unrated opponents

If exactly one roster is in Valve's table, the opponent has no rating. The board then computes the rated side's probability twice: once with the opponent at the official VRS floor (#380, 381 pts) and once with the opponent at the VRS median (740 pts across 380 ranked teams). The published range is those two numbers. The pick is published only if both assumptions agree on the favourite; otherwise the row is withheld. These rows are never counted as verified.

5. Join gates

  • Normalisation strips case, accents, punctuation and generic words (Team, Esports, Gaming, …). No prefix or substring matches: “B8” never matches “B8 Academy”.
  • Published aliases (e.g. Apogee Esports → Apogee Betclic) are applied only with an agreeing opponent and kickoff, and are listed on Flagged.
  • Kickoff tolerance is five minutes. A quote without an established kickoff is never joined.
  • Duplicate Valve rows for one organisation are resolved only with official evidence: five-player roster, newest counted match in Valve's details file; ties are refused.

6. Confidence labels

Strong ≥70% · Solid ≥60% · Lean ≥53% · Toss-up below that · Assumption for rows in the assumption tier. Labels describe the probability, not the quality of the evidence; read the badge for that.

7. Refresh

Every 4 hours (and on demand) the workflow collects HotSpawn, Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Kalshi and Valve's standings, rebuilds this board, runs the contract tests and commits the rendered pages. If the lobby page cannot be read the previous inventory is kept and the failure is recorded in the source ledger instead of publishing an empty board.

8. What is deliberately not done

  • No HotSpawn crowd or pick'em percentages.
  • No scraped or guessed odds; unreachable sources are reported, not estimated.
  • No value is carried forward from a previous board when its quote has expired.
  • No blending of the model into a verified market pick.
