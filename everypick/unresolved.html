3. Structural limitations that apply to the whole board

  • No HotSpawn crowd data. HotSpawn's pick'em percentages are never read; the collector drops those fields. This is deliberate, per the project requirement.
  • HLTV is not machine-readable from automation. Every capture attempts hltv.org/matches and records the answer. At this capture it answered HTTP 403, so no HLTV number is used anywhere on this site. HLTV match pages are linked only where another source identifies the fixture.
  • Valve publishes monthly. The newest official standings file is 2026_10_05; it is verified to be the newest in Valve's repository, so comparisons can lag results by up to a month.
  • Bookmaker coverage is a snapshot. If no book had a market open at capture time, the row falls to the rating model or the assumption rule. It may be priced again on the next 4-hourly refresh — the board rebuilds automatically.
  • Unrated does not mean weak. In this capture UNiTY (VRS #69) is priced at 39.5% against unrated Apogee Betclic, which is why an unrated opponent can never be assumed to be the underdog.
  • Withheld rows are not zero. A withheld pick means "the sources do not support a number", not "the match is a coin flip".
