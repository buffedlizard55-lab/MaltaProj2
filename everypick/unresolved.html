Limitations

L8 · Eight-hour price expiry

Retained prices expire after eight hours. Missing or timezone-ambiguous observation times, future timestamps, and missing or ambiguous market start times are rejected. Rejected prices do not silently survive a failed refresh; a row falls back to a labelled two-rated-team model or an unresolved blank.

Source: https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/MaltaProj2/blob/main/scripts/5f903db4/build.py

L1 · Reference books do not price tier-C CS2

9 of 14 retained fixtures have no accepted price from Bovada, Melbet, Pinnacle, Thunderpick or Kalshi in the retained source captures. Those rows carry an originated line from Valve's official rating table only when both teams have a rating; otherwise they remain unresolved.

Source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events?series_ticker=KXCS2GAME&status=open&limit=200&with_nested_markets=false

L2 · Originated lines are models, not market prices

Formula p = 1/(1+exp(-k*C*points_delta)) with Valve's published C=0.0056000078 and this project's fitted k=0.43077894236569164 (60 market-vs-rating pairs). The fit's own mean absolute error is 7.70 percentage points, so this is in-sample market-fit error, not a confidence interval or demonstrated outcome accuracy.

Source: https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/main/model/fit.js

L3 · Valve's rating table is 16 days old

The newest published global table is 2026_10_05; Valve publishes roughly monthly. Promotion/relegation, roster changes and results after 5 Oct are not reflected. Valve rows also carry a latest_match_date per roster, which is published alongside every rating used here.

Source: https://api.github.com/repos/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/contents/live/2026

L4 · Missing rankings are not probability bounds

No verified rating is available for at least one team in each unresolved fixture. No floor rating is imputed. A new external two-way price or verified ratings for both teams are needed before publishing a pick.

Source: https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/main/readme.md

L5 · Thin exchange book on the one Kalshi-priced fixture

The Kalshi quote used for Zetta v LOS NINOS has zero traded volume and a 0.16 wide two-way spread (Los Ninos 0.55/0.63, Alianza Lima 0.39/0.47). Our VRS origination and bo3.gg's own model both favour the other side, so the row is flagged as a basis conflict and the pick is shown with Lean confidence.

Source: https://kalshi.com/markets/kxcs2game/kxcs2game-26oct211800lnale

L6 · Clock conflicts are not silently joined

Books occasionally list the same pairing at a different kick-off (e.g. Kalshi's Apogee Esports vs UNiTY esports event is priced at 16:30Z while HotSpawn and two other books have 14:00Z). Quotes are joined only when the kick-off agrees within 90 minutes; conflicts are listed on Flagged for review.

Source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/events?series_ticker=KXCS2GAME&status=open&limit=200&with_nested_markets=false

L7 · HotSpawn's own listing is not always complete

Four DFRAG Invitational fixtures render a pick card with no match permalink, and one fixture (Wildcard v DENDELE, 12 Nov) exists in the fixtures API but is not rendered in the lobby and is marked 'started' months early. It is included here as an API-only row so no fixture is dropped.

Source: https://phpstack-1372681-6459848.cloudwaysapps.com/api/v1/fixtures

What would remove these limitations

  • A book posting tier-C CCT/DFRAG/MCG lines (then the row switches to market basis automatically).
  • Valve publishing a newer standings file: the pipeline re-reads the official repository tree and picks the newest file, so ratings update without a code change.
  • Valve publishing a rating row for each team that is unrated today (TitanFlow, Off Chops, Staqued, LOS NINOS, Apogee Betclic) — estimates require verified ratings for both sides.
