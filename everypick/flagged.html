R1 · Valve publishes two rows for the same organisation (different rosters) data

1081597 benched gods v TitanFlow; 1081600 Donstu v Banda chuya; 1082792 HOTU v The Huns. Both rows are in standings_global_2026_10_05.md with different points. This build applies the published roster rule (full five, newest latest_match_date) and keeps the alternative in the row data, so the choice is auditable.

Affected: 1081597, 1081600, 1082792

R2 · Two collectors disagree on the same fixture and one leaves rows blank upstream

data/fe803b80/board.json stores 13 open rows with the top-level pick/probability set to null while their own 'line' or 'model' block holds the numbers, and it also keeps a Kalshi quote whose sides are inverted (Turma do Pagode labelled at 0.78 when the exchange prices Imperial there). This build reads raw decimals and re-derives every no-vig price itself, and it does not import that inverted quote.

Affected: 1082837, 1081870, 1081597, 1081600, 1082688, 1082689, 1082858, 1082859, 1082860, 1082861, 1082792, 1082793, 1080200

Source: data/fe803b80/board.json

R3 · Primary board resolves duplicate Valve rows the other way round upstream

Both collectors mishandle Valve's duplicate rows, in opposite ways. data/f7279a54/board.json flags 'Banda chuya', 'The Huns' and 'benched gods' as ambiguous and therefore publishes no rating for those sides at all; data/fe803b80/board.json instead silently picks the older/lower rows (Banda Chuya #373 402 pts, The Huns #213 701 pts, benched gods #329 516 pts), which flips the Banda Chuya pick. bo3.gg's squad page for Banda Chuya lists exactly Valve's five-man row (#165, 801 pts, latest match 2026-10-04), and Valve's own latest_match_date column agrees, so the five-man row is the active one. Both the selected and the alternative values are published on every affected row.

Affected: 1081600, 1082792, 1081597

Source: data/fe803b80/board.json + https://bo3.gg/teams/banda-chuya

R4 · Kalshi market timestamps are shifted against its own rules text upstream

Two independent events show the same offset: KXCS2GAME-26OCT211800LNALE's rules text says 'Oct 21, 2026 at 6:00 PM EDT' (22:00Z) while its occurrence_datetime says 2026-10-22T02:00:00Z, and KXCS2GAME-26OCT220100RAVA's rules text says 'Oct 22, 2026 at 1:00 AM EDT' (05:00Z) while its occurrence_datetime says 09:00Z - a +4h shift on both. The rules text agrees with HotSpawn, HLTV and bo3.gg, so the rules-text kick-off is used and the event clocks are treated as unreliable.

Affected: 1082837

Source: https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets?event_ticker=KXCS2GAME-26OCT211800LNALE
