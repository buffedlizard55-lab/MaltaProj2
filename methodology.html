Origination Methodology & Line Formulation Mathematical Model

How match probabilities, head-to-head decimal & American odds, map spreads, and tournament outright lines are derived without Hotspawn bias.

Fundamental Rule: Independent Line Origination

Project requirements dictate: "Do not use Hotspawn as an indicator for match probability... We need to originate our own lines for all matches."
To meet this requirement with zero hallucination, we constructed a logistic model anchored in Valve's official Regional Standings (VRS). No prediction-market calibration exists in the code — a previous edition's claim to the contrary was removed (IRREG-012). Momentum bonuses in the script are disclosed model assumptions, not verified facts.

1. Valve Regional Standings (VRS) Anchor

Valve's VRS is the official mathematical ranking system governing CS2 Major tournament qualification. Unlike subjective opinion polls, VRS calculates points directly from prize-pool-weighted match outcomes against ranked opponents.

R_base = 0.75 × VRS(Oct 5) + 0.25 × VRS(Aug 3)

2. Bradley-Terry Logistic Formulation

Fair two-way win probability between Team A and Team B in a standard Best-of-3 (BO3) series is modeled using the generalized Bradley-Terry logistic function:

P_fair(A) = 1 / [1 + 10^((R_B - R_A) / 400)]

3. Market Overround (Vig) Application

To translate fair probabilities into realistic sportsbook betting lines, a standard 4.5% two-way market margin is applied, matching Tier-1 esports market standards:

P_mkt(A) = P_fair(A) × 1.045
Decimal_Odds = 1.0 / P_mkt(A)

4. Map Handicaps & Totals Modeling

In a BO3 series, the probability of a 2-0 sweep scales non-linearly with series win probability. The probability of going the distance (3 maps, Over 2.5) is originated as:

P(Sweep) = P_fair^1.55
P(Over 2.5 Maps) = 1 - [P(Sweep_A) + P(Sweep_B)]
