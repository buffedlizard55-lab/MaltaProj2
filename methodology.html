Methodology &amp; sources

How the fixture list, the quoted odds and the derived probabilities are produced.

Current picks vs. research snapshots. The CS2 and Probabilities tables use eligible primary-bookmaker quotes only; unverified values stay blank. Other linked sections preserve dated research, not live prices. Estimates are not guarantees. 18+; gamble responsibly.

1. Fixture discovery (HotSpawn)

The Pick'ems lobby is an Angular app backed by a public JSON API. The lobby page only server-renders the next ~27 fixtures, so the fixture list on this site comes from the API the page itself calls: https://phpstack-1372681-6459848.cloudwaysapps.com/api/v1/fixtures (plus /tenants/slug/hotspawn for tenant metadata). Fixtures with a cancelled or ended status are excluded; the rest are published with their raw source status, tier, tournament, lock time and the lobby's own crowd pick percentage (reference only). Evidence: evidence/hotspawn_probe.json.

3. What is collected per match

  • the source's own event and market identifiers,
  • the raw quoted prices in the source's own format (and the format name),
  • the retrieval timestamp in UTC,
  • the market type — series/match winner only, no map markets,
  • the source's reported start time and status,
  • the public URL the quote was read from.

4. Derivation

Implied share qi = 1 / decimal_oddsi; bookmaker margin overround = qa + qb − 1; proportional no-vig probability pi = qi / (qa + qb). With two or more sources the dashboard shows their arithmetic mean and the min–max range, and names the sources. These numbers are derived, not official forecasts.

5. Matching rule

Both participants must match after normalisation (order-insensitive; reversed pairs keep reversed prices), and explicit UTC start times must agree exactly. Missing times are not guessed from league names. Quotes and fixture snapshots must be no older than four hours, prematch/open, with confirmed outcome ordering, and the fixture must be upcoming and unlocked. Ambiguous fixture joins or multiple quotes from one source are rejected rather than assigned by input order. Every decision, alias and rejection is recorded in evidence/matching_evidence.json.

6. Cross-session material

This integration keeps every session's artefacts: VRS-originated reference lines, verified fixtures, verified teams, flagged irregularities, team identity evidence, the parallel dashboard and the audit reports linked from the Flagged page.

7. Refresh

A scheduled GitHub Actions job re-runs the collectors, re-derives the lines and rebuilds these pages. The job runs hourly and publishes its data and rendered root pages together. Collection failures are recorded; missing or ineligible quotes leave the probability blank. Eligibility is recalculated on every site render, including Pages staging, so old inputs cannot become current merely by rebuilding HTML.
