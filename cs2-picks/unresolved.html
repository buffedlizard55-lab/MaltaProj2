Why a low-tier match may have no bookmaker price

Reference books (Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Thunderpick) and the Kalshi exchange do not list most tier-C CS2 fixtures. The resolution ladder is applied in this order and every attempt is recorded: featured book lines → other esportsbooks / betting lines → Kalshi exchange → HLTV/bo3.gg identity checks → official Valve VRS origination → labelled bound → explicit unresolved blank. HotSpawn's own crowd percentage is never used.

Current limitations

  1. Reference books do not price tier-C CS2. 9 of 14 open fixtures have no price at Bovada, Melbet, Pinnacle, Thunderpick or Kalshi (all five read today). Those rows carry an originated line from Valve's official rating table instead of a blank. source ↗
  2. Originated lines are models, not market prices. Formula p = 1/(1+exp(-k*C*points_delta)) with Valve's published C=0.0056000078 and this project's fitted k=0.43077894236569164 (60 market-vs-rating pairs). The fit's own mean absolute error is 7.70 percentage points, so treat originated probabilities as +/-8 pp. source ↗
  3. Valve's rating table is 16 days old. The newest published global table is 2026_10_05; Valve publishes roughly monthly. Promotion/relegation, roster changes and results after 5 Oct are not reflected. Valve rows also carry a latest_match_date per roster, which is published alongside every rating used here. source ↗
  4. One-sided ratings become bounds, not points. 3 fixtures have exactly one rated side. The unrated side is at or below Valve's published floor (#380, 381 pts), so those rows show '>=' on the rated side and '<=' on the other. They are directional, not calibrated. source ↗
  5. Thin exchange book on the one Kalshi-priced fixture. The Kalshi quote used for Zetta v LOS NINOS has zero traded volume and a 0.16 wide two-way spread (Los Ninos 0.55/0.63, Alianza Lima 0.39/0.47). Our VRS origination and bo3.gg's own model both favour the other side, so the row is flagged as a basis conflict and the pick is shown with Lean confidence. source ↗
  6. Clock conflicts are not silently joined. Books occasionally list the same pairing at a different kick-off (e.g. Kalshi's Apogee Esports vs UNiTY esports event is priced at 16:30Z while HotSpawn and two other books have 14:00Z). Quotes are joined only when the kick-off agrees within 90 minutes; conflicts are listed on Flagged for review. source ↗
  7. HotSpawn's own listing is not always complete. Four DFRAG Invitational fixtures render a pick card with no match permalink, and one fixture (Wildcard v DENDELE, 12 Nov) exists in the fixtures API but is not rendered in the lobby and is marked 'started' months early. It is included here as an API-only row so no fixture is dropped. source ↗

What makes a row unresolved

  • no book or exchange prices the fixture and neither team appears in Valve's official standings;
  • team identity cannot be confirmed across HLTV / bo3.gg / Valve (name or roster mismatch);
  • the only quote fails the kick-off tolerance or has no two-way price;
  • any re-derivation check in Method fails — the row is then flagged, never silently changed.
