Rules

  • Every open HotSpawn CS2 fixture appears on All picks.
  • A probability comes from an external two-way price, a researched external price, or the official Valve regional standings. HotSpawn's crowd pick percentage is never used.
  • A row with no supported number keeps a blank probability and is listed on Unresolved & limitations. It is never filled with 50/50.
  • Every row links the source it was read from, with the read time.
