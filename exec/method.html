Method, formulas and limits

Short enough to check by hand. Every number on this site is reproduced from the sources listed on the row that shows it.

1. Fixtures

The open list is read from the exact fixture response the public HotSpawn lobby page embeds. A fixture is open while its published lock time has not passed and it has no winner. The crowd "picked %" that the lobby also publishes is refused by the parser: it never reaches a table, a model or a percentage.

2. Prices

Quotes are collected from public, unauthenticated endpoints only: Pinnacle's guest odds API, Bovada's coupon JSON, the 1xBet-platform LineFeed (Melbet / 22bet / Betwinner), Kalshi's trade API and the Thunderpick coefficient bo3.gg publishes per fixture. American odds are converted with decimal = 1 + price/100 (positive) or 1 + 100/|price| (negative); decimal prices are used as published.

3. From a price to a probability

For a two-way market the implied shares are q = 1/odds and the bookmaker margin is removed proportionally: p = qa / (qa + qb). Where several sources quote the same fixture the published market number is the median of the per-source no-vig probabilities, and the minimum, maximum and width are printed. Quotes are joined only when the canonical team names match as a pair and the kick-off times agree inside three hours; anything else is refused and listed on Unresolved & limits.

4. The originated line (only where no market exists)

Where no reachable market prices a fixture, the line is originated from Valve's official global standings: pa = σ(k · C · (Pa − Pb)) with Valve's own Glicko constants and a slope k fitted through the origin on the market pairs seen in the same run (bounds [0.25, 1.5], minimum 6 pairs). This run used k = 0.3912 from 7 pair(s) (fitted). The result is labelled an originated model line on every row; it is not a bookmaker price and no bookmaker is quoting it.

5. What the pick is

The pick is the team with the higher probability. Confidence labels are derived from the distance to 50%: ≥30 points Strong, ≥15 Solid, ≥5 Lean, otherwise Toss-up; an exchange-only row is capped at "thin exchange book".

6. Refresh cadence and no manual input

The GitHub Actions workflow exec-b1fb1465 runs every 4 hours and commits the refreshed data and pages in one commit; nothing in this section needs to be typed in. This build: 11 open fixtures, 150 quotes collected, 11 picks published, 0 left blank and flagged.

7. Reconciliation with the other boards on this site

Different sessions publish different counts for the same lobby, because each counts "priced" by its own rule and its own snapshot time. This section states its rule explicitly: market = at least one licensed bookmaker two-way price joined by team pair plus kick-off inside three hours; exchange = Kalshi order book only; originated = no price; unresolved = no price and no official rating. Rows that another board publishes as blank may therefore appear here as an originated line, and rows another board prices from a source that refuses this runner may appear here as exchange-only or originated. Both facts are printed per row rather than reconciled into a single opaque number.

8. Standing limitations

  • HLTV refuses the collection runner (HTTP 403), so its odds cannot refresh automatically; the refusal is republished on Sources every run.
  • Tier-C events have structurally thin coverage; single-source rows are flagged.
  • Probabilities derived from prices are market observations, not forecasts; originated lines are this project's own numbers.
  • No value is inferred. A missing source leaves the cell blank and the row flagged.
