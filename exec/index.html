Unresolved picks & limitations

A pick is resolved the same way as the featured picks when at least one licensed bookmaker quotes a two-way price for that fixture, which lets the draw-free win probability be read straight from the market. Everything else needs a second-best source or stays blank. The categories below are the whole of that gap; the full page lists every row with its sources and read times.

The three structural limitations of the whole method
  • HLTV refuses the collection runner. Every automated read of hltv.org returns HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare challenge, so HLTV can only be cited as a dated, interactive cross-check — its odds cannot be refreshed automatically. The per-run HTTP status is published on Sources.
  • Tier-C events are structurally thin. Low-tier CCT / Elisa / university events are listed by few books; when coverage exists it is often a single source, which is flagged per row.
  • Estimates are not guarantees. A no-vig probability derived from prices is a market observation, and an originated line is this project's own number.

What a pick is, on this page

The pick is the team with the higher derived win probability, shown in bold with its percentage. Both sides' probabilities are always printed, so the margin is visible. No pick is ever taken from HotSpawn's crowd percentage, and no value is filled in by inference: where a source does not exist, the cell is blank and the row is flagged.
