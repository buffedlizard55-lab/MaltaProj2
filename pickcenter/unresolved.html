Limitations that apply to the rest of the board

1. Most of the list is not pickable. 62 of the 78 fixtures the lobby counts for this week still carry June/July kickoff timestamps ("waiting for information"). HotSpawn lists them and counts them in the week, but their lock time has long passed, so no pick can be entered. They are published here with a research line where one is defensible (labelled "VRS model" or "Lower bound") and listed as unresolved otherwise.
2. HLTV blocks automation. The collector probes hltv.org/matches every run and records HTTP 403. HLTV is therefore used only for identity cross-checks that were read and linked by hand; no HLTV page feeds a probability. Official Valve standings and licensed books/exchange carry every number.
3. Valve's standings are dated 2026_10_05. That is the newest file Valve publishes. Model and bound rows therefore lag results since that date; the date is shown with every rating, and rows switch to a market line automatically as soon as a book posts one.
4. A bound is not a probability. 31 rows are published as bounds because one team has no official rating. The assumption ("the unrated opponent sits at Valve's lowest published rating, 381 pts") is printed on the row; the true value can only be more favourable to the rated team, never less.
5. Single-source prices are thin. Rows priced by one book only are flagged "single source price". A Kalshi price is an exchange mid, not a guaranteed fill.
6. No HotSpawn crowd data. The lobby's "picked %" is not a price and is never used; a test asserts the board never contains it.
