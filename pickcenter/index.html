What this covers, in plain words

The lobby lists 78 Counter-Strike 2 fixtures for pick'em week 273. Of those, 13 are still before their published lock time and 74 resolve to a pick with both probabilities. 8 come from real prices at Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet or the Kalshi exchange; 35 are originated from Valve's official Regional Standings because no book posts a price on them yet; and 31 are published as explicit bounds because one side has no official rating.

The rest of the list — 62 fixtures — are listed by HotSpawn with June/July kickoff times that it never updated ("waiting for information"). They are not pickable, but they are kept here with a research line where one is defensible, and named as unresolved where it is not. That is the difference between a list that says "13 of 78" and one that says "78 of 78, here is what each one is".

Standing limitations: HLTV blocks automated reads (HTTP 403), so it is used only for identity cross-checks; Valve's newest published standings file is dated 2026_10_05, so model rows lag recent results; and a bound is a floor or ceiling, not a point estimate. All three are spelled out on the unresolved page.

How a pick is made

  1. Market first. Every verified Pinnacle / Bovada / Melbet / Kalshi price that agrees on both team names and the start time (five-minute tolerance) is de-vigged and averaged; the pick is the higher of the two probabilities.
  2. Then origin. With no external price, both teams' official Valve ratings give the probability, using a slope fitted to 60 observed book lines (k = 0.4307789423656918).
  3. Then bound. With one team unrated, the rated team's chance is published as a lower bound with the assumption written on the row.
  4. Otherwise blank. No number is invented, and the row is listed as unresolved.

HotSpawn's own crowd percentages are never read, never stored and never used as a probability. The lobby tells us which matches exist; the numbers come from books, the exchange and Valve.

Sources. Fixtures and lock times: the official HotSpawn lobby (play.hotspawn.com/lobby). Prices: Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet and the Kalshi exchange, each linked on the row it prices. Ratings: Valve's official Regional Standings (github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings). HotSpawn crowd percentages are never used.
