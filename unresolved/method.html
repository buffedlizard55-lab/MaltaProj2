Method & sources

How each pick on the every-match page was produced, in the order the project brief requires, and what each label means.

The featured route (tried first for every match)

An eligible two-sided primary-bookmaker quote (Pinnacle, Bovada) joined to the fixture by both team names after normalisation and the exact UTC kickoff, prematch and open, with confirmed outcome ordering, a unique join, an unpassed pick lock and a quote/fixture snapshot no older than four hours. Rows that meet it are labelled Featured bookmaker line and are not re-derived here.

The fallback ladder (only for matches the featured route cannot resolve)

  • 1. HLTV. Identity and ranking evidence read from official HLTV match, team and analytics pages, archived with read times in data/7177d07f/hltv_research.json. HLTV 'Best Odds' columns were empty for every unpriced fixture inspected on 2026-10-14, so HLTV contributes identity and ranking — not a price. HLTV's logged-in user-vote split is recorded but never used as a probability, exactly like HotSpawn's crowd percentage.
  • 2. Esportsbooks / esports betting lines. Melbet's public LineFeed, Thunderpick coefficients as published by bo3.gg, and Kalshi exchange books. A price is used only when both team names match the fixture; the no-vig probability is p = (1/odds) / Σ(1/odds).
  • 3. Official Valve VRS. Points from Valve's own counter-strike_regional_standings file, read through the GitHub API, with Valve's published Glicko expected-win curve and the slope re-fitted against this snapshot's market-priced fixtures (k = 0.5136, 1 pairs, MAE 0.1123).
  • 4. Published VRS standing substitution. Where a team appears in no Valve file row but HLTV publishes its Valve VRS standing, points are substituted from the file's standing-order position. This is a documented substitution, flagged rating_from_published_standing_not_a_measured_rating.

This collector runs on GitHub-hosted runners. When a source cannot be read it is recorded as unreachable and the build falls back to the last committed snapshot; a source failure never becomes a guessed number.

Limitations

  • Originated lines are model output with an official rating input. They are not bookmaker prices and no bookmaker is quoting these matches.
  • Valve's published file is a snapshot (2026_10_05, 380 rows); rosters and ratings move between snapshots.
  • HLTV's republication of the Valve VRS rank and Valve's own file can disagree because they are different snapshot dates. Both are published where they differ.
  • Team identity is the largest source of error on the tier-C events. Every alias used is listed with its evidence and its counter-evidence on the Unresolved & contested page.
  • The ladder is applied to this run's lobby inventory. A new low-tier fixture with no rating and no price anywhere would appear as Unresolved rather than be filled in.
