Standing limitations of this board

  • The sandbox this session works from has no general internet access, so the dated capture is produced by a GitHub-hosted runner and the manual source reads are recorded with the URL and the UTC date they were read.
  • HLTV answers HTTP 403 to the automated probes used elsewhere in this repository; HLTV is therefore used here as a research source (results, rosters, rankings) that a human read and cited, never as an automated odds feed.
  • The official Valve standings snapshot is dated 2026_10_05. Teams whose name appears twice in it (two different rosters) are treated as ambiguous unless an official lineup source disambiguates them.
  • Pinnacle's public guest endpoint carried only 7 CS2 matchups in this capture, so most low-tier matches rest on Melbet's line feed alone.
  • HotSpawn crowd percentages are deliberately not collected and never enter a probability.

Not affiliated with HotSpawn or any bookmaker. HotSpawn supplies fixtures and settlement only; its crowd pick percentages are never used as a win probability. Every probability on this site is our estimate from the cited external lines, not an official forecast.
