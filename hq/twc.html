What day 2 and day 3 tell us for day 4

  • The day-1 upset did not repeat: Aurora beat 9z 2-0 in the Group A decider (Cache 13-11, Inferno 13-4) and dropped Nuke, the map they had lost 13-1. The 9z result reads as a bad day, not a new ceiling.
  • Rest: FURIA and Falcons both won their winners' matches on 15 Oct and had 16 Oct off. Aurora and BETBOOM both had to play their group decider on 16 Oct. The rest edge sits with FURIA in semi-final 1 and with Falcons in semi-final 2.
  • Route: BETBOOM played four matches to reach the semi-final (2-1 PARIVISION on 15 Oct, then 2-1 Legacy on 16 Oct) against Falcons' two. More matches also means more recent map veto information on BETBOOM.
  • Where the market and the read disagree: HLTV ranks FURIA #4 and Aurora #6, yet the books have FURIA-Aurora as close to a coin flip (51/49 on this capture) and HLTV tags Aurora the bookmaker favourite. The market is pricing Aurora's form and the head-to-head win, not the ranking gap.
  • Contrarian-but-not-stupid: the obvious contrarian side is Aurora in semi-final 1 only in the sense that the ranking gap suggests FURIA - the market already agrees with FURIA, so FURIA is not contrarian here. The genuinely contrarian-but-supported side is BETBOOM in semi-final 2, and it is a 24% shot: the form and the rest both point the other way, so it is a leaderboard play, not an edge.
