Method & sources

Line ladder (in order)

  1. Esportsbook/exchange prices — Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Kalshi, Thunderpick (via bo3.gg mirror), egamersworld. Remove the margin: fair = (1/odd) / Σ(1/odd). Average across books; the published line may blend with the model (board weight 0.75–0.85 on market rows).
  2. Alias re-join — a dropped book quote counts when the team-name variant is justified by agreeing opponent + kickoff (e.g. 'Betclic' = 'Apogee Betclic' for #1081546).
  3. HLTV team research — ranks, rosters, form, verified results. Used for direction on even lines and for context notes. HLTV user votes are a crowd, not a line — never a probability.
  4. Valve VRS originated line — only when no price exists: standings snapshot 2026-10-05 (official ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings) + the canonical board's fitted calibration (k=0.25, mae≈0.06181442214818696 on 14 pairs).

HotSpawn supplies fixture discovery and crowd ownership only. Its percentages never enter any probability.

Pick rules

  • The pick is the argmax of the published fair probabilities. If the market line is exactly even, the pick is a documented research tiebreak and confidence is Toss-up.
  • Climb pick: our fair ≥35% on the underdog side, HotSpawn crowd ownership on that side ≤22%, crowd sample ≥20. Below 35% → 'no climb'.
  • Blank is allowed only when the whole ladder fails; blank rows must publish the attempt ledger. In the current capture: 0 blanks.
  • Every table row links an official source. Values that can't be confirmed stay blank and are flagged on flagged.html — never inferred.
