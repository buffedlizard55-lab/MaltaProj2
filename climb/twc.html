How to climb (not stupid, not chalk)

What the 9z 2-0 Aurora upset teaches about climbing the leaderboard

  • L1 Freshly rebuilt rosters carry a low floor early: Aurora's July rebuild was 3 events old and they lost 13-1 on Nuke — system collapses happen to top-5 teams. A dog with a live star and nothing to lose can take a bo3. src
  • L2 Contrarian does not mean 20% longshots. Both TWC upsets were dogs in the ~25-35% band (9z opening, BETBOOM semi). The climb threshold used here is >=35%: below that the hit rate does not pay for the leaderboard risk. src
  • L3 Crowd ownership is the actual leaderboard lever: a 60% favourite picked by 90% of the crowd is a bad 'safe' pick; a 55% side picked by 9% is the climb. Use HotSpawn percentages as ownership only. src
  • L4 Form fragility is real: Falcons were 12-9 up on Dust2 in the semi and lost the series. Post-collapse favourites in dead-rubber matches are the natural place for a conditional underdog shot. src
  • L5 Rest alone did not decide day 4 (rested Falcons lost, rested FURIA won). Rest is context, not a pick. src
