Method & sources

Every match, not invented coverage

HotSpawn's public lobby supplies the fixture inventory only: CS2, scheduled, lock in the future, present in the captured lobby transfer state. API-only historical rows are not counted as current lobby picks. Crowd percentages are excluded. Every captured open match remains listed even if its probabilities are blank.

Market first

Both teams must match and the explicit source kickoff must agree within five minutes. Missing, approximate or ticker-inferred starts are rejected. No-vig probability = (1 / decimal odds) / sum of both inverse prices. Consensus weights are Pinnacle 2, other accepted sources 1: a disclosed modelling choice. Raw prices are re-derived, not stored probability fields. Market picks are never blended with fallback ratings.

Fallback only for unpriced matches

Valve's official standings provide rating inputs, not verified win probabilities. Both names must resolve to unique rating rows. Our expected-win estimate uses Valve's Glicko curve with slope k=0.348; 12 priced/rated fixtures were available for fitting. With fewer than six, the uncalibrated default is explicitly retained. No missing roster is replaced by siblings or the lowest-rated team. Ambiguous identities and unverified HLTV aliases remain blank.

Freshness and automation

Fixture observations (including HTTP cache Age) and quotes expire after four hours. Ratings expire after fourteen days. These are conservative project policies, not source guarantees. Each card expires at its earliest evidence deadline or lock. Refresh is scheduled every thirty minutes; GitHub delays and blocked sources are possible. A failed lobby read retains the original observation time, never resets it.

Source access

Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet and Kalshi are attempted by the existing collector. HLTV and Polymarket access failures or absent markets never justify made-up lines. Missing inputs and review flags are published. Verification scope and source links.
