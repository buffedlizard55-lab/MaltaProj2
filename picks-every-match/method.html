Method & sources

Coverage and automatic refresh

The collector reads the public HotSpawn lobby Angular transfer-state /api/v1/fixtures response. Only CS2 rows actually listed on that page enter this board; the broader API cannot add or overwrite lobby entries. Open means scheduled, before kickoff and pick lock at evaluation. Retained data keeps its original observation and cache age after a fetch failure. HotSpawn crowd percentages are discarded.

The automatic workflow is scheduled at minutes 12 and 42 hourly (faster than the requested 4–8 hours). Source errors and Actions delays are possible; schedules are not guarantees. Cache-adjusted fixtures and quotes expire after four hours. Valve snapshots expire after 14 days. These are project safety thresholds, not source claims.

1. External market first

Both team names and a timezone-qualified start within five minutes must agree; missing/approximate kickoff, live markets, stale quotes, ambiguous rematches and unknown participants are rejected. Probabilities are recalculated from raw prices, never trusted from stored derived percentages. Each source contributes once. Decimal no-vig P(A) = (1 / odds A) / ((1 / odds A) + (1 / odds B)); consensus weights Pinnacle ×2 and other sources ×1. That weighting is a modelling choice. Eligible market results are not blended with ratings.

2. Rating fallback only where no eligible market exists

Both teams need unique entries in the official Valve standings. We apply a logistic transform based on Valve's published expected-score formula, RD=75, with slope k=0.3544. Calibration fits logit(market probability) against point difference for 12 eligible pairs in this snapshot. With fewer than six pairs, k=1 (uncalibrated). This is our estimate, not Valve's official match prediction or validated predictive accuracy. Roster changes, BO1/BO3 and map vetoes are not modelled.

3. Missing evidence stays blank

No other team's rating is assigned to an unrated team. No 50/50 fill, guessed floor, thin-book or one-participant slot join is permitted. HLTV can support identity research for uncovered fixtures; unavailable pages are logged, not invented. RoundsGG → ROUNDS is documented by HLTV's team page; disputed WBT/WBT Academy labels remain unresolved. Every row has an official fixture link and every consumed market/rating input has its source link.
