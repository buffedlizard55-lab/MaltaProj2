Method & sources

Fixture inventory (what “every match” means)

Fixtures come from the public HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby (play.hotspawn.com/lobby): the Angular transfer-state embedded in the page (its /api/v1/fixtures response) merged with the same public fixtures API the lobby calls, CS2 only, scheduled with lock time in the future at collection time. HotSpawn is used for discovery only. Its crowd “picked %” is never copied into the snapshot and never used as a probability. The collector re-runs automatically on GitHub Actions (about every 30 min); a failed HotSpawn fetch keeps the previous inventory and flags it, never an empty board.

Featured resolution (tiers A/B) — tried first for every match

  • Bookmaker/exchange prices: Pinnacle guest API, Bovada coupon JSON, Melbet LineFeed, Kalshi KXCS2GAME and Polymarket Gamma. Kalshi and Polymarket books enter tier A only when the bid/ask spread is ≤10 percentage points; wider books are retained as labelled fallback/corroboration. A featured price joins only when both team names match after published normalisation/aliases and source start time is within tolerance. No substring matches.
  • Polymarket discovery: the collector queries Gamma's numeric Counter-Strike match tag 100780, filters to active full-match sportsMarketType=moneyline / “Match Winner” contracts, rejects Yes/No props and map markets, and stores each exact event URL, source labels, outcome prices, spread, volume and retrieval time. The older tag_slug=counter-strike probe was incomplete and is no longer used.
  • No-vig / normalization: bookmaker odds use p_i = (1/odds_i) / Σ(1/odds_j); binary exchange prices are normalized by the same two-way formula. Consensus = weighted mean (Pinnacle ×2, others ×1).
  • Valve VRS model: official points from ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings 2026_10_05, Valve's own Glicko expected-win formula with its slope re-fitted to observed market prices (k = 0.410, fitted on 15 priced fixtures this snapshot; k=1 would be uncalibrated Valve). Market+model blend: 85% market / 15% model (≥2 sources) or 75/25 (1 source), in logit space — a stated modelling choice, not a source value.
  • HLTV first for uncovered identity research: direct HLTV fixture/team pages are linked as schedule/roster evidence, never interpreted as betting probabilities. Automated reachability is logged; a block or 403 leaves values blank rather than fabricating data.

Fallback resolution (tier C) — only where A/B is impossible

  • Elisa slot prices: HotSpawn's WROTBERRY label conflicts with HLTV/Liquipedia schedules and direct markets that say WBT/WhiteBIT Team. A line may guide tier C only when competition family, start and the other opponent all match. Source names are preserved on the row; no WBT VRS row is assigned to WROTBERRY. Wide Polymarket prices contribute only to the displayed evidence range when a tighter Melbet line exists.
  • Identity conflict excluded: Kalshi calls those slots WBT Academy. Since published WBT and WBT Academy rosters differ, its midpoint is shown on Flagged for Review but is not averaged and does not expand the probability range.
  • LFO 10 / Sökerorg exact-roster line: two HLTV LFO 10 match pages and a Liquipedia Sökerorg roster page independently list dottie, flaw, tvs, Sapec and Jackinho. A direct market under Sökerorg may therefore orient a tier-C LFO 10 pick, but the missing explicit rename statement remains flagged and prevents a featured join.
  • Thin books: exact-name Kalshi books and Polymarket contracts rejected from featured prices for spread >10 points may be used only in tier C, with raw market quality retained. They are never described as tight/featured lines.
  • RoundsGG alias: RoundsGG → Valve VRS ROUNDS (548 pts) is a documented roster/name mapping and every affected row is flagged; it may support a VRS-model row.
  • Originated sibling/floor VRS (last resort): if real HLTV/market research still leaves one side unrated, a same-prefix sibling range or the official file floor can keep the user-requested every-match list complete. Such a value is confidence-capped and explicitly originated. It is not used for LFO 10 while the direct exact-roster market is available.

Every C pick is confidence-capped (“Low”), ranged, flagged, and listed on Flagged for review with its evidence links. If no evidence of any kind exists (tier D), the probability is left blank — never invented.

Verification

Unit tests (tests/test_c64a5e19.py and tests/test_57e26db7.py) independently derive the open inventory from the committed HotSpawn snapshot, parse captured Gamma-shaped records, re-check every probability/tier, require source links, and enforce the identity fail-closed rules above. The workflow runs them before publishing.

Limitations and unresolved picks

Probabilities are estimates, not verified outcomes or betting advice. Prices move; prediction-market books can have little volume or wide spreads, both shown where available. Elisa group matches are Bo1, so variance is higher. WROTBERRY↔WBT and LFO 10↔Sökerorg remain name-identity irregularities rather than confirmed renames. Every affected pick stays tier C on Flagged for Review. Fallback picks are materially weaker than A/B picks; if no documented evidence exists at all, tier D leaves the probability blank rather than inventing it.
