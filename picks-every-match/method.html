Method & sources

1. Every fixture, not just featured matches

Read the Angular transfer-state fixtures embedded in the public HotSpawn lobby. Use the published CS2 game identifier and preserve all lobby-listed rows. A scheduled match is open only before its published lock. HotSpawn is never a probability input.

2. Market first

Try public Pinnacle, Bovada and Melbet full-match, two-team prices. Join both participants and source kickoff (maximum five-minute discrepancy); reject live markets, props, unknown/approximate times and stale quotes. The retained provider response must reproduce every consumed price and participant pair before publication.

For decimal odds dA and dB, our fair estimate is pA = (1/dA) / ((1/dA) + (1/dB)); pB = 1 − pA. Combine accepted sources with a declared weight of 2 for Pinnacle and 1 for others. This weighting is a modelling choice, not an official win probability. Market picks are not blended with rating estimates.

3. Fallback research only for market gaps

Research HLTV first; the captured probe and manual research limitations remain visible. Then try other esportsbooks/exchanges. A Kalshi full-match fallback requires two active binary outcome contracts, matching teams, consistent explicit originally-scheduled CS2 match rules, a verified EDT/EST/UTC timezone, and two usable bid/ask books (spread at most 10 cents each). A ticker, occurrence time, expected expiration or settlement deadline is never a kickoff. Retained official JSON must reproduce both rules and prices offline. These normalized yes midpoints are estimates, not executable odds; volume can be low or zero. This fallback is used only for market gaps and never changes an accepted featured/bookmaker estimate.

Use two uniquely identified teams in Valve’s official VRS standings (snapshot 2026_10_05; refuse duplicate roster names and unproven aliases. Standings older than 14 days are ineligible. Apply a logistic rating-difference model using Valve’s Glicko code and fit parameters; fit the slope to eligible priced fixtures in this snapshot when at least six pairs exist.

Current slope k=0.5340; eligible calibration pairs=14; fitted=True. A model-only estimate is not a bookmaker line, not a verified probability, and has no claimed out-of-sample accuracy. No sibling/floor ratings, ambiguous tournament-slot joins, or default 50/50s. Missing inputs stay blank.

4. Expire, flag, retry

Quotes and cache-adjusted fixture observations must be at most four hours old. Preserve last-known inventory on fetch failure, but never reset its evidence age. A backup timestamp cannot replace missing, failed, invalid or future retained HTTP metadata. When both HTTP and backup observation clocks exist, the earlier one and unchanged cache Age bound eligibility. Recheck all sources every four hours with GitHub Actions, and publish only after tests and source replay succeed. Schedule delays, caches and provider blocks are reported, not hidden.
