Limitations that apply to this list

  • Coverage, not absence. A missing price means "not found in the sources read at this timestamp", never "no market exists". Each row is retried by the 4-hourly collector.
  • Timing. Tier-3 books typically post within 24 hours of kickoff; the two VOID Autumn Cup rows are more than 48 hours out at build time.
  • Identity. Two HotSpawn cards name an opponent that no other source agrees with (Lavked vs "LFO 10" — books and bo3.gg say Sokerorg). Those rows are priced against the source-agreed opponent and carry an opponent name conflict chip. Verify the HotSpawn card before locking.
  • Lower bounds are not point estimates. A "≥ x%" row says only that the favourite is at least that likely given the opponent cannot be rated above Valve's published floor.
  • Ownership is not probability. Crowd shares come from HotSpawn's rendered cards and are used only in the leverage column.
