Known limits of the method

  • Originated lines carry model error: the fitted curve misses the market by 6.1% on average across the pairs it was fitted on, and it is unvalidated out of sample.
  • The exchange tier requires a two-sided Kalshi book with a spread of 10¢ or less and a kick-off stated in Kalshi's own rules text; thin books are rejected and listed on Flagged.
  • Where two official entries share a team name, the rule "highest-rated entry listing at least four players" is applied and both candidates are published above so the reader can disagree.
  • Prices age: rows whose only price is older than four hours keep the probability but are flagged quote_older_than_4h rather than silently refreshed.
  • No source is ever HotSpawn for a probability, and crowd ownership is excluded from every formula.
