Irregularities found while verifying this build. None of them blocks a pick; all are published so they can be checked by hand.

F1 · Aurora had no October 2026 roster change

The brief states there was a recent Aurora roster change. HLTV and bo3.gg both list the same five starters and the same coach, and neither shows an October move. Premise not used in any price.

https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora

F2 · HotSpawn names the wrong opponent for Lavked

HotSpawn card 1079634 reads 'Lavked vs LFO 10'. Bovada, Melbet and bo3.gg all price 'Lavked vs Sokerorg' in the same CCT Europe Series 11 slot. LFO 10 does not appear in Valve's list (only LFO 7/8/9). The row is priced from the books but flagged.

https://bo3.gg/matches/lavked-vs-sokerorg-17-10-2026

F3 · Duplicate team names in Valve's official list

Valve's 2026-10-05 list contains two rows each for Banda Chuya, ex-RUSTEC and 6666. The capture correctly refused to guess. This desk disambiguates by roster (full five-man row, and for ex-RUSTEC the row sharing three of bo3.gg's five players) and records the rule in research.json.

https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/main/live/2026/standings_global_2026_10_05.md

F4 · WROTBERRY cannot be proven to be 'WhiteBIT Team'

Melbet prices 'WhiteBIT Team vs Nexus Gaming' at the same kickoff as Nexus vs WROTBERRY. No source read here proves the names are the same team, so the price is not joined; only a lower bound is published.

https://bo3.gg/teams/wrotberry

F5 · Two TWC prices disagree by a full coin-flip

The three books in the canonical capture put FURIA at 51.1%; Thunderpick has the semifinal at 1.85/1.85 (50.0%). The desk publishes the three-book consensus and shows the second source on the row. It does not average away the disagreement silently.

https://bo3.gg/matches/furia-vs-aurora-gaming-17-10-2026

F6 · HotSpawn renders times in UTC-4

Every lobby card this session rendered in America/Sao_Paulo (e.g. the 14:00Z semifinal showed as 10:00). All times on this desk are converted to UTC and cross-checked against the canonical capture's start_utc.

https://play.hotspawn.com/lobby
