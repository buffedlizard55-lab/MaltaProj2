What the 9z upset actually teaches

On day 1, 9z beat Aurora 2-0 (Inferno 13–11, Nuke 13–1). On day 3 the same two teams met in the decider and Aurora won 2-0 (13–11, 13–4). That is the honest lesson: one Bo3 upset is not evidence the market is wrong, and the rematch result corrected the picture within 48 hours. Do not fade the price. Fade the crowd. The edge comes from matches where the price is close but the crowd is one-sided — which is exactly the FURIA–Aurora semifinal.

The Aurora roster-change premise

Checked on HLTV and bo3.gg: both list XANTARES, woxic, Jimpphat, kyxsan, Wicadia with coach ash (Ashley Battye). No October 2026 roster change is visible on either source. The rebuild is older (July 2026 window, as recorded by earlier sessions). The premise is therefore flagged rather than used to move a price — see Flagged for review.
