Method, sources and limits

Resolution ladder (in order)

  1. Market. Full-match two-way moneyline prices from Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet and the Thunderpick coefficients published by bo3.gg (the esportsbook tier for matches no sharp book covers), read from their public endpoints. No-vig probability = (1/dec_a) / (1/dec_a + 1/dec_b). Multi-book rows use the weighted mean with Pinnacle weighted 2× (declared modelling choice). A price prices a row only if the source's own names match the fixture and its stated kick-off is within five minutes of HotSpawn's.
  2. Exchange. Kalshi series KXCS2GAME: the yes-bid/yes-ask midpoints of both sides, normalised, accepted only when both sides are two-sided, the spread is ≤ 10¢, and the market's own rules text states the kick-off (converted from the zone it prints).
  3. Originated (official ratings). Where no bookmaker prices a match: this session's line from Valve's official regional standings, P(A) = σ(k · C · (points_a − points_b)) with Valve's own formula (C = g·ln10/400 at RD 75) and the slope k fitted by least squares against the market lines in the same capture. This build: k = 0.444 from 21 pairs, mean absolute error 5.9% (Valve's own k = 1 would miss by 11.8%).
  4. Unresolved. Nothing is guessed: the probabilities stay blank, the pick stays blank, and the row is published with its blocker on Unresolved picks.

Contrarian (climb) rule

  • Safe pick = higher probability side. Climb pick = safe pick unless the underdog clears 30% win probability and 2.0× leverage (probability ÷ crowd ownership of that side).
  • At most 3 activations per UTC day, ranked by leverage × offered points; other qualifiers keep the favourite and still print their gate results.
  • Crowd ownership comes from the lobby's own picked percentages. It is never a probability, never part of a price, and is printed as a percentage of picks only.
  • Exact ties (a market that prices both sides the same): the safe pick follows the official rating model when one exists, the climb pick takes the less-owned side at zero expected-point cost.

Refresh cadence

Collection, resolution, page rendering, verification and tests run every four hours in .github/workflows/desk-6484426f.yml (also on demand), inside the 4–8 hour policy. Prices older than 4 hours are flagged rather than silently reused.

Limits

  • 21 calibration pairs is a small sample; the originated lines are the weakest tier and are labelled as such on every row.
  • HLTV is read manually by this session (it returns 403 to automated collection); every HLTV fact used here carries its URL and read time in data/6484426f/research.json.
  • Kick-off times published by the lobby are unlabelled US-Eastern; the reconciliation is published on Flagged.
  • Probabilities are model estimates from prices, not guarantees, and no profit is implied.
