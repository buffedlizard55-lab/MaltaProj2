Method & sources

1. Inventory

Fixtures, points, tiers and lock times come from the canonical capture data/c64a5e19/board.json (collected 2026-10-17T09:27:01Z → 2026-10-17T09:27:14Z), which reads the public HotSpawn lobby. This desk does not re-invent fixtures; it joins to that capture by fixture id.

2. Probability

For decimal odds dA and dB: pA = (1/dA) / ((1/dA) + (1/dB)), i.e. the no-vig two-way price. Multiple books are combined with weight 2 for Pinnacle and 1 for the others (a modelling choice, declared). The live Thunderpick read is used as a second source and shown next to the consensus; it never silently replaces it.

Model rows use Valve's official published rating points and the same shrink curve as the canonical board: u = 1/(1+10^(-Δ/400)), p = 0.5 + (u-0.5)·0.4213, where Δ is the rating-point difference. It is an estimate, not a bookmaker price.

Lower bounds put the unrated opponent at the floor of Valve's published list (381 pts, rank 380) and therefore report the minimum probability of the rated side. It is written "≥ x%" and never presented as a point estimate.

3. Picks and the leaderboard rule

  • Pick = the side with the higher probability. On a true 50/50 the pick is the lower-owned side, labelled "priced tie".
  • Leverage = probability − crowd share, for that side. Positive means the field is under-weighting the side.
  • Climb pick requires all three: the side is the underdog, its market probability is at least 35% (the "not stupid" floor), and its leverage is at least 20 points. No climb pick is invented when the thresholds fail.
  • Points per match come from HotSpawn (2 / 3 / 7) and are shown on every row. The leaderboard impact of a pick is roughly points × (probability − crowd share); that is the arithmetic behind the "Aurora is the one real edge" call.

5. Refresh

Rebuilt every 4 hours by .github/workflows/desk-3e1be203.yml, and after every new canonical capture. A rebuild re-reads data/c64a5e19/board.json; it does not invent a new observation. If the capture is stale, the page says so with the capture's own timestamps.

6. What this desk does not do

  • It does not use HotSpawn crowd percentages as probabilities.
  • It does not fill a blank with 50/50, a sibling-team rating or an average of unrelated matches.
  • It does not claim a model estimate is a bookmaker price.
  • It does not edit another session's board or data.
