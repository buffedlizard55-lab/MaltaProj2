Reproducible, separate layers

Estimate first. Choose risk second.

1. Canonical probability, not crowd popularity

A listed day-three match copies its accepted canonical win estimates exactly. For decimal odds dA / dB, pA = (1/dA) / ((1/dA) + (1/dB)); pB = 1 − pA. Accepted source weights are 2 for Pinnacle, 1 for other primary providers. Qualitative results/roster notes do not alter the price. Full strict source, identity and freshness gates.

2. Unlisted tournament preview

Primary bookmaker JSON is independently re-parsed from the retained official responses. Both participants, a sourced kickoff within five minutes, prematch state and observation age at most four hours are required. The preview has no synthetic fixture ID, ownership or offered points. It cannot become a HotSpawn pick. Retained primary response payloads.

3. Optional one-swing leaderboard rule

  • Listed, accepted external-market match with at least 2 primary bookmaker sources.
  • Underdog win estimate at least 35%; verified rounded HotSpawn ownership above zero and at most 20%.
  • Probability / ownership at least 2×. A displayed zero is not treated as exact zero ownership.
  • At most one deliberate underdog swing across the two day-three deciders. Missing evidence means no swing, not a coin flip.

This is a declared heuristic, not a backtest, a predicted contest rank, a bankroll recommendation or a proof of contest-optimality. The prior 9z upset is already known; it is not an out-of-sample successful pick claimed by this review.

4. Show the tradeoff

If a correct pick awards the same displayed m points to either side, expected match points are m × p. Sacrificed expectation for an underdog = m × (pFavorite − pUnderdog). Probability / ownership is only a descriptive leverage ratio, not expected profit or a guaranteed leaderboard edge. Correlation, ties, future matches, payout structure and the user’s current leaderboard position are not modelled.

5. Evidence and expiry

Ownership is read from the exact retained official lobby fixture in canonical participant order and never supplied to the probability parser. Forecasts expire at the earlier of source deadline and match lock; re-rendering never refreshes source clocks. The browser hides expired estimates, while dated result/roster facts remain readable. Automatic four-hour updates are best effort.
