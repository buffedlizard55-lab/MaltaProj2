Aurora opening upset: context, not a probability rule

9z beat Aurora 2–0 on the opening day, then Falcons beat 9z 2–1, and Aurora beat 9z 2–0 in the Day-3 rematch. Aurora later lost 0–2 to FURIA in the semifinal. These are verified results, not proof that an upset changes the base rate for the next fixture. The roster rebuild was announced in July, around 98 days before the opener; no arbitrary last-minute penalty was added.
