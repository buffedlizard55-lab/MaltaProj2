Odds conversion and probability method

For American odds +x, decimal return is 1 + x/100 and raw implied probability is 100/(x+100). For −x, decimal return is 1 + 100/|x| and raw implied probability is |x|/(|x|+100). Decimal return includes the stake. For each two-way market, de-vig by dividing each raw implied probability by the sum of both sides. This simple proportional (multiplicative) method produces one-book market estimates; it does not create a market consensus.
