Market lineMedian fair (no-vig) probability across real bookmaker/exchange prices collected this run. Strongest evidence.
Our line · VRS modelNo book prices this match. Probability originated from both teams’ official Valve VRS points (Valve’s Glicko formula, slope fitted to 96 observed market pairs, typical error ±7pp). An estimate — labelled as one.
Direction onlyOnly one side has a verified rating and no book prices the match. We pick the rated side and leave the % blank rather than invent one.
UnresolvedNo price and no usable rating. No pick is published — see the limitations instead of a guess.
