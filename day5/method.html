What this desk is

A phone-first board of Counter-Strike 2 picks for every match the HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby lists, with a win probability for each side derived from sources outside HotSpawn, and a separate page for the TWC 2026 day-5 final.

How a probability is derived

StepRule
1. DiscoveryFixtures come from the HotSpawn lobby page and the public HotSpawn fixtures API, which is the same API the lobby itself calls. A fixture is "open" if it is on the lobby and its lock time has not passed.
2. PriceA price comes from Thunderpick (through bo3.gg), Pinnacle, Bovada or Melbet. The margin is removed proportionally: p = (1/dA) / (1/dA + 1/dB).
3. JoinA price is joined only when both team names resolve to the same canonical key and the source kickoff is within the tolerance window. Names are folded for accents, organisational words (Gaming, Team, Esports) and a leading "ex" (a rebranded roster). A one-sided price is rejected.
4. BlendWhere more than one book prices the same match, the no-vig probabilities are averaged with equal weight per book.
5. ModelWith no external price, Valve's own expected-win formula is applied to the 2026-10-05 global regional standings, through the repository's own engine, with the slope fitted to observed bookmaker lines. Labelled as a model, never as a price.
6. BoundWith only one side rated, a conservative lower bound is published and labelled as a bound.
7. UnresolvedWith neither side rated and no price, the row is published blank on the unresolved page with the reason and the sources that were tried.

The ratings model, and how its slope was fitted

Valve's published rating curve is pA = 1 / (1 + 10−g·Δ/400), with g from Valve's own Glicko code and Δ the rating difference. Valve's own README says that curve is too steep — it underestimates win rates at the low end and overestimates them at the high end — so the slope is learned from observed bookmaker lines instead of assumed. Least squares on logit(market) = k·C·Δ over the market pairs below gives the k used on every model and bound row.

Fitted slope k: 0.43078
Market pairs used: 60 (minimum needed: 6)
Mean absolute error: 0.077 with the fitted slope, against 0.1225 with Valve's raw slope

Rows where a book price and the ratings model disagree by 20 points or more carry a model_market_gap flag, and the row says so. The price always wins; the model is the fallback.

How the pick is chosen

Every row carries a safe pick, which is simply the side with the higher probability, and a leaderboard edge layer, which is the side the crowd under-picks. The leaderboard edge is only taken when all three of these hold, and the reason is printed when it is refused:

  • our own probability for that side is at least 35% — below that it is a lottery ticket, not a climb;
  • our probability beats the crowd's share of that side by at least 5 points;
  • the crowd has not already taken that side (it must be at most 15% picked).

When any of those fail, the row says so and why, so a refusal is as reviewable as a pick.

What is never used

  • HotSpawn's crowd percentages as a probability. They are context for the leaderboard decision and nothing else.
  • Any value that cannot be traced to a source. A blank cell is the correct answer, and it is published as blank with a reason.
  • The bo3.gg rating field as a win probability. It repeats across every match of a tournament, so it is a tournament metric, not a per-match forecast.
