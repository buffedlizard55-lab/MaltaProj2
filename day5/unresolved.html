Limitations of the method

  • HotSpawn is not a source of probability. Its crowd percentages are shown as leaderboard context only and never enter a probability. A crowd split measures what people picked, not what is likely to happen.
  • A probability is only ever one of four things. A real external bookmaker price with the margin removed; an exchange midpoint, labelled as such; Valve's own regional-standings model when no book prices the match; or a conservative lower bound when only one side is rated. The basis label on every row says which.
  • The ratings model is not a bookmaker price. It applies the expected-win formula Valve publishes for its own ranking to a dated snapshot of the global standings. It is a model, it is twelve days old, and it is labelled as a model wherever it appears.
  • Low-tier coverage depends on the esportsbook, not the mainstream books. Pinnacle, Bovada and Melbet do not list most CCT and NODWIN events. The lines that cover them come from Thunderpick through bo3.gg, and where that is missing the row falls back to the ratings model or to the unresolved list.
  • Name matching is conservative. A price is joined only when both sides of a fixture resolve to the same canonical team key and the source kickoff is within the tolerance window. A one-sided price, a price with no kickoff, or a price with a different opponent is rejected rather than guessed at.
  • Exchange and prediction-market prices are midpoints, not executable quotes. Kalshi's bid/ask midpoint and a Polymarket midpoint are shown where they exist and are labelled; where nothing else prices the match they may be used under the same rule-backed fallback the canonical board already applies, and the row says so.
  • Nothing is forecast for a match that has not started. These are pre-match probabilities from prices and ratings. They are not results and they are not advice.
  • The Valve snapshot is dated. The newest global standings file in Valve's repository is 2026-10-05; there is no live feed, so a roster or ranking change after that date is not reflected in a model-based row.
  • Coverage is a snapshot, not a subscription. The board is rebuilt every four hours from a fresh read of the lobby and the books. A match that appears between reads waits for the next one.
