0. Current pipeline safety and archived-source limitations

Current picks require fresh primary-bookmaker quotes, exact kickoff identity, explicit game/market state, confirmed outcome ordering, an unlocked upcoming fixture and unique joins. A rejected quote is not a confirmed line. The parallel current/ board has an unnamed third-party fallback and a separate source/identity policy; it is a dated research snapshot, not this pipeline's current confirmed picks. Its reversal handling needs owner review (derived probabilities in lines.json are already fixture-oriented). The prior third-party combined probabilities, hypothetical VRS lines, pickem board and independent reports are preserved research snapshots, not current picks. They have not all been re-verified in this session. Sources for manual review: official lobby, HLTV analytics, Pinnacle, Bovada, pipeline audit and limitations.

Numeric legacy HotSpawn links such as /matches/1079438 were tested and redirect to a generic match listing, not a verified match permalink. Current rows use a match permalink only when that exact URL was published in the lobby state; otherwise they use the official lobby and carry an official_match_permalink_unavailable flag. No slug is synthesized.

Coverage is incomplete wherever no eligible official quote exists. No claim is made that every match is priced, that a retrieval is real-time, or that a derived probability is a verified fact. Sandbox egress does not allow these bookmaker hosts; automatic retrieval runs in GitHub Actions. Per-attempt payloads and failures: collection evidence.

10. Known limitations

  • Only full-match / series-winner moneyline markets are used. Map markets, handicaps and totals are excluded.
  • An exact source-published UTC start is mandatory. A missing or different start is rejected, never matched by tournament name.
  • Derived probabilities use proportional no-vig normalisation — a disclosed modeling choice, not an official forecast.
  • Bookmaker prices can move after the row's read timestamp; in-play markets are never eligible.
