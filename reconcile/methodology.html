Method & sources

  1. Fixtures: HotSpawn public lobby API record (data/hotspawn_matches.json, derived from the lobby) — teams, kickoff, lock time, points. Crowd pick % is not read.
  2. Quotes: every board's published quote (Pinnacle, Bovada, egamersworld, Kalshi) is re-read from the board JSON files below. The newest quote per source is kept.
  3. Orientation: quotes are aligned to the HotSpawn team order by normalised team name; a board whose names do not match either order is skipped and flagged.
  4. Arithmetic check: when decimal odds are published, the proportional no-vig probability p = (1/oa)/((1/oa)+(1/ob)) is recomputed and must match the published number within 0.2 pp, else the quote is excluded.
  5. Eligibility: not live, retrieved ≤ 240 min before build, has a source URL.
  6. Consensus: arithmetic mean of eligible bookmaker/aggregator probabilities (Pinnacle, Bovada, egamersworld). Kalshi is reference only. Pick = higher probability; label Strong ≥ 70%, Lean 55–70%, Toss-up < 55%.
  7. Open: HotSpawn status scheduled and lock time after build time; your browser also greys out rows that have locked since the build.
  8. Blank rule: no eligible quote → no probability, no pick.
