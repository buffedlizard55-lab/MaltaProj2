Methodology

Exactly how each number was produced, and what was excluded.

Pipeline

Time base: every kickoff in this dataset is UTC, taken from the lobby payload's scheduled_start epoch values (row-level evidence in audits/1d4de836/lobby-snapshot-audit.json) and matched against the HLTV match page. Hotspawn's rendered clock reads four hours earlier (US Eastern), and Polymarket's clocks for the Elisa BreakOut markets read four hours earlier again - both deviations are published on the Flagged for review page rather than silently corrected in the data.

  1. Enumerate. The Pick'em lobby (play.hotspawn.com/lobby and /cs2-pickems) was read at three points in time. Every fixture that was open for picks was recorded with its tournament, tier, points, kickoff (UTC), both team names and the crowd percentages.
  2. Verify the fixture. Each fixture was matched to an HLTV match page (kickoff, format, both team names). Where no HLTV match URL exists yet, the HLTV event listing and a second independent site (bo3.gg) are cited instead, and the gap is flagged.
  3. Capture external prices. Two independent markets are used:
    • Bookmaker: EGamersWorld's Bets on <event> by bc.game module on each event page (every EGamersWorld odds module states the bookmaker it prices).
    • Prediction market: Polymarket CS2 markets for the same fixture.
  4. De-margin. For a two-way market with prices o1, o2: p_i = (1/o_i) / (1/o_1 + 1/o_2). Prediction-market prices are normalised the same way p_i = c_i / (c_1 + c_2), which removes the bid/ask spread.
  5. Our line. Equal-weight average of the de-margined probabilities from the sources captured for that fixture. Fair odds are 1/p. Where no external two-sided price was captured the row is left blank and flagged - no value is guessed.

What is deliberately NOT used

  • Hotspawn crowd percentages. They are displayed in their own column, labelled "context only", and are excluded from every calculation. They are a measure of what other players did, not of the match.
  • Model/AI win probabilities (for example bo3.gg's "prediction" percentages). They are not market prices and are not used for the line.
  • Unsourced inference. If a value was not captured with a link, the cell is empty and the item appears on the Flagged for review page.

Why the numbers will move

Prices are point-in-time. Two things were observed inside the capture window: (1) the captured decimals changed between page components and between fetches, and (2) the market sum (overround) differs by fixture, from roughly 1.02 on liquid Tier S matches to ~1.35 on empty Tier C prediction markets. Every row therefore carries a quality badge derived from the captured spread, and the capture date is stated in the header of every page.

Reproducing this build

The entire site is generated from scripts/build_pickem_lines.py, which holds the captured values next to the URL they came from. python3 scripts/build_pickem_lines.py regenerates data/pickem_pool.json and every page in pickem/. Data files: pool.json.
