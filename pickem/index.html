What this is

Every CS2 fixture that was open for picks on the Hotspawn Play Pick'em lobby was captured, matched to an external betting market, and converted into our own de-margined probability. Nothing on the Hotspawn side is used as a probability: the crowd "picked" percentage is displayed in its own column and is excluded from every calculation.
