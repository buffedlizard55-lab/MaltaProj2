Summary of the limitation. Low-tier Counter-Strike fixtures are frequently listed by HotSpawn but not priced by any bookmaker or exchange we can read. For those the pick comes from Valve's official rank values, which are published monthly — the current file is 2026_10_05 — and from a fitted conversion of the point gap into a probability. That conversion has a mean absolute error of 7.7 points of probability on the 60 market observations it was fitted on, so treat originated rows as estimates rather than prices.

5 · Sources that could not be read, and what that costs us

HLTV — https://www.hltv.org/matches returned HTTP 403 (HTTPError 403). HLTV publishes match pages and results rather than two-way prices, and its odds panels are populated by partner bookmakers only for covered events; the low-tier fixtures here have no HLTV odds panel. No number on this site depends on HLTV.

Polymarket — https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?closed=false&tag_slug=counter-strike returned no CS2 match markets at capture time, so that venue is not represented. Polymarket's coverage of low-tier CS2 is thin; a listing there would be used as an exchange price (rung 2) if both names and the kickoff joined.

Thunderpick is an esportsbook whose prices are visible through bo3.gg match pages. It priced the market rows in earlier sessions' captures and added nothing that Pinnacle, Bovada or Melbet did not already price, so it appears in the source ledger but is not a separate rung at this build.

What would remove each limitation: a bookmaker opening these matches (low-tier CS2 lines typically appear in the last 12–24 h before kickoff, so re-running nearer the lock is the single most useful action); a published rating for the unrated team; or a roster document confirming which of Valve's two rows is being fielded.
