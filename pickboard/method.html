1 · The HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby, reverse engineered

3 · The arithmetic

De-vig. For decimals d_a, d_b: p_a = (1/d_a) / (1/d_a + 1/d_b). A book whose implied sum falls outside 0.95–1.30 is rejected as bad data rather than trimmed.

Valve's curve. Valve publishes its model: Q = ln(10)/400, g = 1/sqrt(1 + 3·Q²·RD²/π²) with RD = 75, and expected score 1/(1 + 10^(g·(r−r₀)/−400)). That is a logistic in the point gap with slope C = g·ln(10)/400 = 0.0056000078.

The fitted slope. A straight read of that curve is too steep (Valve's own README says so). We fit k in logit(p) = k · C · Δpoints by least squares through the origin on 60 (fixture, market consensus) pairs captured by this repository: k = 0.430779 (raw 0.430779, bounds [0.25, 1.5]). Mean absolute error 7.7 probability points; Valve's un-fitted slope scores 12.2 points on the same sample. Residual sd 9.3 points — that is the honest error bar on every originated row.

Duplicate rosters. 2026_10_05 publishes one row per roster. Policy: drop rosters with fewer than five players; if two five-player rosters remain, take the one whose official details page shows the most recent counted match; if they tie, leave the fixture unrated.

Contested rows. If a market price and the rating model differ by more than 15 points the market is still used (it is a price), but the row is labelled contested and listed on the Unresolved page.

Refresh cadence: cron: '17 */4 * * *' in .github/workflows/pickboard-86f3af65.yml, plus a manual dispatch. The workflow fails loudly if the board comes out with a blank row, a probability that does not re-derive, or a HotSpawn-only citation.
