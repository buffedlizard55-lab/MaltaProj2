How the picks are produced

One ladder, applied in order, never blended across rungs:

  1. External betting lines — Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet (licensed bookmakers) and Kalshi (regulated exchange). Both team names must agree and the kickoff must agree within five minutes; a two-way price is de-vigged proportionally. 4 of 12 fixtures priced this way.
  2. Originated line from Valve's official Regional Standings — when no book prices the match, the official point gap is converted to a probability with a slope fitted on this repository's own 60 market-observation pairs (k=0.4308, MAE 7.7 pts). 5 fixtures.
  3. Originated line with a floor proxy — where one team is absent from all four Valve files, it is assigned the lowest rank value Valve publishes (381 points, #380). Explicitly an assumption. 3 fixtures.
  4. No pick — published blank with the sources attempted, never guessed.
