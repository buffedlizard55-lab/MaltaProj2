How this is built

Method & sources

Where the fixtures come from

The HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby (play.hotspawn.com/lobby) is a client-rendered page. The fixture list it renders is served by a public JSON endpoint the page itself calls: /api/v1/fixtures (~9.6 MB, 12,000 rows across all sports). The collector keeps CS2 rows, resolves the current week's fixtures and joins them to the individual match pages linked on every row (https://www.hotspawn.com/matches/<slug>-<fixture_id>). Lobby week at capture: 273, 2026-10-19T00:00:00.000Z → 2026-10-25T23:59:59.999Z. Kick-off times are UTC; each fixture also carries a lock time six minutes before kick-off, which is what the cards sort by.

HotSpawn is used for discovery, kick-off times and settlement only. Its crowd pick percentage is not read.

Research ladder used for un-priced matches

  1. HLTV — fixture identity, rosters, ranking, head-to-head and map form (hltv.org/matches). HLTV publishes no usable odds for these fixtures.
  2. Esportsbooks / betting lines — bo3.gg (which republishes Thunderpick coefficients), kushvsporte.ru odds boards, egamersworld (rejected: 1.80/1.80 placeholders), Polymarket (thin markets rejected as prices).
  3. Official Valve VRS — ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings, the same file Valve publishes; the exact file and its date are cited on every rating source.

Verification performed on these rows

  • Fixture identity cross-checked against the HotSpawn match page and HLTV's match page (same teams, same UTC kick-off).
  • Every quoted price re-read from the page that publishes it; decimal odds stored verbatim, then normalised in code.
  • Every ratings-based number re-derived from the numbers printed in Valve's file (points, not ranks).
  • Every row in All picks carries at least one https source link; the tests check this mechanically.
  • Probabilities are checked to sum to 1 and the pick is checked to equal the higher-probability team.

Refresh

The automated collector runs every 30 minutes on the repository's schedule and rewrites the upstream board; this section rebuilds every 4 hours (and on every push) from the freshest capture, so a new HotSpawn fixture appears without manual input. If the capture is older than 6 hours the summary shows a staleness warning instead of pretending to be live.

Reproduce it

python3 scripts/e1b48771/build.py reads the freshest data/*/board.json, applies scripts/e1b48771/resolutions.json and rewrites cs2/ plus data/e1b48771/board.json. python3 -m unittest tests.test_e1b48771 re-checks the published pages against that data.
