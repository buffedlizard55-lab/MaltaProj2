3 · Limitations of the whole method

  • No price means no quote. Where no bookmaker, exchange or Thunderpick-via-bo3.gg price is reachable, the number is originated from the official Valve regional standings table (VRS). It is a model output, not a market.
  • Valve's newest published standings are dated 2026_10_05 (official repo). Valve publishes roughly monthly, so roster moves after that date are invisible to the model. Verified at read time: no newer file exists.
  • Unrated teams are bounded, not rated. A team absent from Valve's table is at or below the weakest published rating (381 pts on 2026-10-05). Any probability for a fixture involving such a team is a lower bound for the rated side, and is labelled as such.
  • Thin prediction markets are not prices. Polymarket map markets quoted 0.40 bid / 0.87 ask on $10–$14 volume are recorded as corroboration only and never averaged into a probability.
  • Aggregated best-price lines mix books. Where one site shows the best available price per side from two different bookmakers, the no-vig normalisation is an estimate of the market rather than a single book's line (flagged per row).
  • Matching is exact. Names are never merged without a recorded decision; a fixture with an ambiguous name keeps its own row (see Flagged).
  • Snapshot, not a stream. Odds move. Every row carries the read time of its quotes; a page rebuild is not a new observation.
  • HotSpawn is discovery and settlement only. Its crowd pick percentage is never read, stored or used, so it cannot move a probability in either direction.
