Overview / Method

How a fixture becomes a pick

A deterministic evidence ladder keeps markets first, identity joins strict, and low-evidence scenarios visibly different from direct lines.

Resolution ladder

Stop at the first supported level

  1. Preserve strict featured marketUse the upstream two-way, no-vig line unchanged when exact team pair and kickoff matching succeed.
  2. Attempt current HLTV research for strict gapsThe current matches probe is evaluated first. At this capture it returned HTTP 403, so no numeric HLTV claim was inferred.
  3. Research exact-fixture sportsbook linesA spelling alias needs the exact opponent, exact kickoff, competition family, raw re-price validation and at least two independent books.
  4. Use two-sided official Valve VRSBoth names must map uniquely—or a current external rank must select one exact duplicate official row.
  5. Publish only ambiguity-robust VRSIf duplicate rows remain, every candidate combination must pick the same winner. The closest-to-50% edge is shown.
  6. Label one-sided floor sensitivityThe unrated side is set to the lowest official VRS points. This is directional and never labelled as a market.
  7. Leave unsupported rows blankNo direct evidence means no invented probability. The row moves to Unresolved picks & limitations.

Exact transform

Valve points → probability

P(A) = sigmoid(k × C × (VRS_A − VRS_B))

Here C = g(75) × ln(10) / 400 and k = 0.43077894236569164. The scale was fitted on 60 contemporaneous exact-pair market observations; mean absolute probability error was 7.7 points. It is calibration—not team-performance data.

Fixture universe

Lobby-listed OPEN rows inside HotSpawn's current CS2 week. API-only started/cancelled conflicts are excluded and logged.

Market conversion

Raw two-way odds are independently converted and overround removed. Pinnacle weight is 2; Bovada, Melbet and Thunderpick weight 1.

Identity

Unicode/punctuation normalization may establish exact names. Semantic aliases are prohibited except an explicit, multi-book exact-fixture rule.

Freshness

The fixture observation must be no older than 4 hours. The dashboard refresh workflow targets every 4 hours and keeps the page's as-of time visible.

Forbidden input: HotSpawn crowd ownership.

Ownership percentages are popularity, not estimated match win probability. The capture fails if known crowd-field names enter its retained input.

Lock and display policy

Rows are published in lock order. Client-side display marks a card locked when the browser passes lock_utc; the scheduled rebuild replaces settled rows. A lock label does not imply live-game pricing.
