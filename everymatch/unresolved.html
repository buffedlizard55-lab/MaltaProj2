5. Standing limitations of this board

  • Valve snapshot age. Valve's newest published global standings is 2026_10_05 (published about monthly). Every VRS-model / floor number is relative to that snapshot, which is ~14 days old at capture; roster moves since then are not reflected.
  • Model, not market. VRS-model and floor picks are our own logistic on Valve's points with a slope (k = 0.4308) fitted to bookmaker lines — an estimate, weaker than a live price.
  • HLTV not machine-readable here. The automated runner cannot parse HLTV betting pages, so HLTV is not a numeric input; Valve's official standings are used instead.
  • Low-tier coverage. Obscure teams are often absent from both bookmakers and Valve's top-380; those rows can only be directional or unresolved.
  • Not betting advice. Estimates only; verify against a live book before acting.
