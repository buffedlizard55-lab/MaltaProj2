Method

Short version: featured market picks use external prices with the margin removed. Matches with no eligible price are resolved from Valve's official standings: a calibrated rating model when both teams are rated, and a labelled directional estimate when only one is. HotSpawn is never a probability input and no price is ever invented or mis-joined.

1. Which matches

Every CS2 fixture open for picks in the HotSpawn lobby, read from its public fixtures API by the OURLINE collector. HotSpawn tells us which matches exist and when they lock; its crowd pick percentages are not read.

2. Featured market picks (unchanged from OURLINE)

Eligible external prices (Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet, Kalshi) are de-vigged (p = (1/d) / (1/d_A + 1/d_B)) and combined as a source-weighted consensus. A price is joined to a fixture only when both team names match after normalisation (or a published alias) and the source start time is within ±5 minutes; in-play prices are excluded. These 15 rows are copied through unchanged.

3. Valve VRS model (both teams rated)

Win probability from Valve's official points via p_A = 1/(1+10^(−k·g·Δ/400)), where Δ is the points difference and g = 1/sqrt(1+3·Q²·RD²/π²) with RD = 75 (Valve's own model/glicko.js / model/fit.js). Valve's readme notes its curve is too steep, so the slope k = 0.4308 is least-squares fitted to 60 eligible priced fixtures (source: ourline f7279a54 fit on eligible priced fixtures). This is the same model and the same fitted k the OURLINE board uses, so a model pick here equals the pick OURLINE would publish if its Valve snapshot were inside its 14-day window.

4. Directional floor estimate (one team rated)

When only one team is officially rated, the pick is that team and its probability is estimated against an opponent assumed at the VRS floor (#380 / 381 pts — the weakest ranked team). Because the unrated opponent's true strength is unknown, this is labelled Directional and listed under Unresolved & limitations. If the unrated name shares a token with an official team (e.g. a renamed org), that is flagged as a possible alias that could change the pick.

5. Duplicate names & aliases

Valve can list two rosters of one organisation. We resolve to the roster with the most recent counted match (tie-broken by higher standing), read from the official details file, and flag it. Acronym aliases (e.g. "Aurora YB" → "Aurora Young Blud") are applied only when the target is unambiguous in the official standings, and are flagged.

6. Automation

.github/workflows/everymatch-19d6c262.yml re-reads the newest OURLINE board and the official Valve standings, re-derives every pick, re-renders these pages and commits. No manual input.
