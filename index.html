Closest matches with eligible source-linked lines

The HotSpawn pick'em lobby lists CS2 (and LoL) matches you can pick. This dashboard collects those fixtures from the lobby's own public API, then looks up external betting lines for every fixture at external bookmakers (Pinnacle and Bovada). Prediction-market snapshots are excluded from current picks. For each match where an external price was found you get the quoted odds, the bookmaker margin and a derived no-vig probability, each with the exact source link and the time it was read. Matches where no external line could be confirmed are left blank and flagged unverified — they are never estimated.

Derived probabilities — the short version

Raw quoted decimal odds are converted to implied shares q = 1 / odds, normalised to remove the bookmaker margin: p = q / (qa + qb). Where two sources quote the same match, the dashboard shows their mean and the min–max range. These probabilities are derived from market prices, not forecasts, and the rule is published on every row.

HotSpawn's own "picked %" is a crowd pick percentage. It is excluded from this table and is never used as a probability input, per the project requirements.
