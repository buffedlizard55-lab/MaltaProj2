What this is

This dashboard reads the exact CS2 fixture response embedded in the public HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby, then looks up external betting lines for every listed match at Pinnacle and Bovada. Prediction-market snapshots are excluded from current picks. For each match where an eligible external price was found, the table shows the quoted odds, bookmaker margin and derived no-vig probability with the exact source link and read time. Matches where no eligible external line could be confirmed stay blank and flagged — they are never filled from HotSpawn or guessed.

Want a pick for every open match? The All picks · every match board (session fe803b80) publishes a pick and a probability for every fixture in the lobby's CS2 list — external bookmaker/exchange prices where they exist, plus a clearly labelled originated line (official Valve VRS rating, or this project's own Elo over published bo3.gg results) for the tier-C matches no reachable bookmaker prices. Every row carries its source link and the basis it uses, and there is a track record. The earlier Our line board (session f7279a54) stays available as preserved research with its own method and track record; model estimates are kept apart from the strictly source-priced tables here.

Executive summary — fastest route to all picks (mobile): Every-match picks at a glance (session c64a5e19) lists every open CS2 match with a pick and win probability on one phone-friendly page, including low-tier matches with no normal bookmaker price, which are resolved there only from documented fallback sources and flagged individually. Start there if you just want the picks; return here for the strict view.

Derived probabilities — the short version

Raw quoted decimal odds are converted to implied shares q = 1 / odds, normalised to remove the bookmaker margin: p = q / (qa + qb). Where two sources quote the same match, the dashboard shows their mean and the min–max range. These probabilities are derived from market prices, not forecasts, and the rule is published on every row.

HotSpawn's own "picked %" is a crowd pick percentage. It is excluded from this table and is never used as a probability input, per the project requirements.
