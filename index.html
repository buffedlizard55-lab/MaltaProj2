Automatic updates

The canonical collector is scheduled every four hours at 00:12, 04:12, 08:12, 12:12, 16:12 and 20:12 UTC. It rediscovers lobby fixtures and retries external markets without manual input. GitHub scheduling, caches and provider blocks can delay updates; this is not an uptime guarantee. Quotes expire after four hours or at pick lock. Source failures preserve inventory without resetting evidence age.
