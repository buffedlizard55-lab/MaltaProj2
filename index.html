How to use this dashboard

  1. Choose a match: open All CS2 picks, then search a team or tournament.
  2. Read the line: market prices first; a labelled model only where no accepted market exists.
  3. For leaderboard risk: use the TWC decision sheet. A possible upset is not automatically a good pick.
