What this is

This dashboard reads the exact CS2 fixture response embedded in the public HotSpawn Pick'ems lobby, then looks up external betting lines for every listed match at Pinnacle and Bovada. Prediction-market snapshots are excluded from current picks. For each match where an eligible external price was found, the table shows the quoted odds, bookmaker margin and derived no-vig probability with the exact source link and read time. Matches where no eligible external line could be confirmed stay blank and flagged — they are never filled from HotSpawn or guessed.

Want a pick for every open match? The separate Our line board (session f7279a54) gives a probability and a pick for every open CS2 fixture, including ones with no eligible bookmaker price. It blends Pinnacle, Bovada, Melbet and Kalshi prices with a Valve VRS rating model, labels which basis each pick uses, and grades its past picks on a track-record page. It is a model estimate, so it is kept apart from the strictly source-priced tables here.

Derived probabilities — the short version

Raw quoted decimal odds are converted to implied shares q = 1 / odds, normalised to remove the bookmaker margin: p = q / (qa + qb). Where two sources quote the same match, the dashboard shows their mean and the min–max range. These probabilities are derived from market prices, not forecasts, and the rule is published on every row.

HotSpawn's own "picked %" is a crowd pick percentage. It is excluded from this table and is never used as a probability input, per the project requirements.
